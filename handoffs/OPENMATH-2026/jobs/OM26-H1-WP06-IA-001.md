Hence relint(AB) contains no arrangement vertex, and AB is an elementary segment.

Because M and N are nonparallel, they intersect at a unique point C = M cap N. The point C lies in exactly one open half-plane bounded by L; therefore no triangular cell bounded by L, M, N can exist on the opposite side of L.
