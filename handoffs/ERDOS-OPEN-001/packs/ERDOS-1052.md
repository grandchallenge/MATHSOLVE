## Protected problem statement

Are there only finitely many unitary perfect numbers?

Protected facts that may be used without re-derivation:
1. The protected formalization records that all unitary perfect numbers are even.
2. It contains exact small examples 6, 60, 90, 87360 and the much larger known example 146361946186458562560000.
3. For n = ∏ q_i with pairwise coprime prime powers q_i, the unitary-divisor sum is naturally attacked through ∏(1+q_i).

No pack member may infer that the problem is solved, easier than other Erdős problems, novel to GCL, or certified. The protected registry status is intake evidence, not MATHCERT authority.
