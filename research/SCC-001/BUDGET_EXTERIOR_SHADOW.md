## 1. Reverse gate elimination with a common budget

Let B be the Boolean function ring on n inputs, V the starting feature space containing 1 and coordinates, F_d(V) the span of products of at most d elements of V, and A=gr_F B. Any history of at most t gates may be padded by redundant zero gates to t. Introduce formal Boolean variables T_1,...,T_t representing the successive outputs. Each gate T_j has definition U_j V_j where U_j,V_j are affine combinations of initial features and T_i for i<j. The final output is a single affine form in the initial features and all T_i. For proof one may first replace a basis of initial features by independent formal Boolean variables, then evaluate on the true inputs; no independence of actual features is needed.

Eliminate T_j in descending order in a product of k affine forms. Each factor involving T_j is T_j+l_i with l_i independent of T_j. The Boolean identity
    (T+l)(T+m)=(T+l)(1+l+m)
follows by idempotence. Consolidate all T_j-dependent factors against the first, so at most one factor contains T_j and the total factor count remains k. Substituting T_j=U_j V_j now replaces the product by the XOR of two products: one of at most k+1 affine factors (replace T_j+l by U_j V_j), the other of at most k (replace by l). If no factor contains T_j there is just the original product. Zero or redundant factors are permitted. The identity is valid in the Boolean ring and allows unrestricted gate reuse, including repeated occurrences in a factorization.

Label the first branch by an increase, the second (or the sole branch) by no increase. Each branch of the full elimination tree is determined by the subset of gate indices where it takes the increase; some subsets need not occur. Starting with one factor, after t eliminations every branch labelled by j increases is a product of at most j+1 initial forms, and there are at most binomial(t,j) such branches. Therefore every f in the terminal state V_t satisfies the exact necessary representation statement
    f = sum_{J subset [t]} p_J,    p_J a product of at most |J|+1 elements of V,
where missing terms are zero. This is an upper bound on the number of terms, not a sufficient condition for a circuit: all factors arise from common gate definitions, and independently chosen products can violate that compatibility.

For d>=2, define the truncated atom demand tau_d(f;V) as the minimum number of products of available forms whose XOR agrees with f modulo F_{d-1}(V). Empty sum allowed. This finite quantity is independent of a gate history. For t>=0 define
    C(t,d)=sum_{j=d-1}^t binomial(t,j)
(empty sum zero). Then
    tau_d(f;V) <= C(t,d) whenever f in V_t.
More precisely the products can be chosen with at most t+1 factors. For d=2 this recovers the exponential order of the old atom compression bounds; for d close to t+1 the truncated tail is much smaller. It supplies no efficient general computation of tau_d. At d>t+1 all output functions lie in F_{d-1}; at t=0 the formula gives zero as required. For affine input features tau_d and the analogous bounded-factor demand are exactly spectator invariant by cylindrical lifting and restriction to unused coordinates zero (degree filtration and factors restrict without increase).

## 2. Exact maximal-layer coset characterization

For t>=1, V_t is contained in F_{t+1}(V). All gates except possibly the t-th lie in F_t(V) by section 1 applied to their prefixes. Thus the image L of V_t in A_{t+1}=F_{t+1}/F_t has dimension at most one. If nonzero it is spanned by the image of a single product of t+1 initial features: only the all-increase branch can contribute. Equivalently its generator is a nonzero decomposable (t+1)-fold product of degree-one symbols in A (the image of a decomposable exterior tensor, since A is generated in degree one and squares of positive homogeneous elements vanish).

Conversely each such nonzero symbol is attained in exactly t gates by successively multiplying its t+1 chosen factors. The zero image is attainable by padding. Hence the possible maximal-layer images are EXACTLY zero and these decomposable-image lines, for arbitrary starting V and t. If A_{t+1}=0 the statement reduces to the zero image.

Fix f of relative degree t+1 and let h be its leading symbol. The projected distinguishing coset is
    Lambda_t^top={lambda on B: lambda(F_t(V))=0, lambda(f)=1}.
Each such lambda restricts to a functional on A_{t+1}; every functional there extends to one on B annihilating F_t. After any t gates the surviving projected coset is exactly its intersection with the annihilator of L. Thus possible maximal-layer surviving cosets are exactly:
    the full coset (L=0);
    the empty coset (L=span(h), possible iff h is a decomposable image);
    a cut lambda(p)=0 for a decomposable-image p independent of h, containing exactly half of the coset.
Here lambda(p) denotes evaluation of the induced quotient functional. These are exact projected cosets, not all full cosets Lambda(V,f), and no other maximal-layer cuts are possible. If h is not a decomposable image, at least half of Lambda_t^top survives every t-gate history, certifying f not in V_t. If h is decomposable, the projected coset can be extinguished but lower-degree obstructions can remain: extinction of this projection alone is not target completion.

At the initial affine state A is the exterior algebra on the n-dimensional space E of linear forms. Decomposability of a nonzero homogeneous d-form h can be tested without enumerating gates: D(h)={w in E: w wedge h=0} has dimension at most d and equals d iff h is decomposable. Extend a basis of D(h) to E; every monomial of h must contain all basis factors of D(h), since wedge multiplication by each factor is injective on monomials omitting it. If dim D=d, only one monomial remains. This is a linear-algebra obstruction on the target symbol, independent of enumerating circuits. It is not an iff criterion in a nonlinear quotient A; there use decomposable-image membership directly.

## 3. Intermediate layers and quantitative joint cuts

For 2<=d<=t+1, project to B/F_{d-1}(V). At most t-d+2 gate generators contribute: every earlier gate lies in F_{d-1}. Denote their projected span L_d. For lambda in the initial coset annihilating F_{d-1} and evaluating f to one, survival is exactly annihilation of L_d. If f+F_{d-1} is outside L_d, the surviving fraction is 2^(-dim L_d), at least 2^(-(t-d+2)); if inside, all are killed. If f already lies in F_{d-1}, the initial projected coset is empty and there is no test. This is a joint cut, not independent witness activation. Each projected gate of index j>=d-1 is a sum of at most C(j,d) products modulo F_{d-1}, by section 1, and every vector in L_d has truncated atom demand <=C(t,d) by applying the same theorem to its affine output. Thus every reachable projected subspace is contained in that bounded atom-sum set. These are necessary conditions, not sufficiency or independent choice of the projected generators.

At one unit of slack, t=d and f has relative degree exactly d, the unique branch with d+1 factors has zero leading symbol in A_{d+1}, since all other branches lie in F_d and f does too. In an affine starting state that branch is either zero or the indicator of an affine subspace of codimension <=d, and its degree-d symbol is either zero or decomposable. The other d branches of d factors also have decomposable-or-zero degree-d symbols. Hence an affine-state target of degree d computable with d gates has exterior rank (minimum number of decomposable d-forms summing to h) <=d+1. No claim of this rank bound in arbitrary quotient A or larger slack; higher-grade terms can cancel and contribute lower symbols.

An independent necessary demand estimate at the affine state is the normalized Hamming distance to degree-(d-1) functions, denoted dist(f,F_{d-1}). A product of affine forms is zero or the indicator of an affine subspace of codimension r, with polynomial degree exactly r: an invertible affine change of coordinates maps it to a product of r coordinates, and affine substitution in either direction does not increase degree. Its normalized support size is 2^-r. Discard all low-degree branches of the reverse elimination representation; the XOR of the remaining branches has support contained in the union of their supports, each of size fraction <=2^-d. Therefore
    dist(f,F_{d-1}) <= 2^-d C(t,d).
This is a degree-sensitive approximation obstruction, not a proof that the optimal approximant is easily found. All statements here use uniform input mass; no analogous probability bound for arbitrary nonlinear V is asserted.

## 4. Small explicit coupled witness certificates for saturated budgets

At the affine state fix f of degree d>=2 and its nonzero exterior symbol h. Suppose h is not decomposable. We construct at most d^2+2 fixed target-disagreeing witnesses, each annihilating F_{d-1}, that cannot all be extinguished by any d-1 gate history. This is a deterministic algebraic certificate, not a union bound over circuits.

Choose a coordinate d-subset I with h_I=1. Any decomposable p with p_I=1 is the wedge of d row vectors normalized to have the identity on columns I: take any factor matrix and row-reduce its nonsingular I submatrix. Over F2 all nonzero determinants are one. Its remaining matrix entries M_{i,j} for i in I, j outside I are exactly the coefficients p_{I-i+j}. For any d-subset J with k=|J minus I|, its coefficient is the determinant of the k-by-k submatrix of M with rows I minus J and columns J minus I (expand the identity columns). The single-replacement coefficients of h determine one normalized matrix M_h. If every remaining coefficient agrees with its corresponding determinant, h equals the wedge of that matrix, contrary to hypothesis. Thus some J, necessarily k>=2, has h_J not equal to the indicated determinant of M_h.

Let c_K be the coefficient functional on the full Boolean ring extracting the square-free degree-d monomial K. It annihilates F_{d-1}. Include c_I, and for each of the k^2 replacement subsets K needed by that determinant and the subset J include c_K if h_K=1, or c_K+c_I if h_K=0. Every included functional evaluates f to one. If a t=d-1 history kills all these witnesses, its maximal-layer image cannot be zero, and its generator p must have p_I=1 and p_K=h_K at every prescribed position. The determinant identity for p contradicts the chosen violation for h. Thus one of these at most k^2+2<=d^2+2 coupled witnesses survives every such history. It is not necessary that a single witness survive all histories; the family is budget-specific. The determinant certificate is obtained from the degree-d coefficient array of f, which may itself be exponentially costly to acquire from an implicit target. No polynomial-time SAT lower bound is asserted.

Explicit sharp family: with d-2 independent variables z_1,...,z_(d-2) and four additional variables a,b,c,e, set
    f_d=(product_i z_i)(ab+ce).
Its degree is d, with leading symbol the sum of the two displayed monomials sharing the z factors. Choose I={z_i,a,b}, J={z_i,c,e}; the four replacement coefficients vanish but both diagonal monomials have coefficient one. Six witnesses suffice, for every d>=2, to exclude d-1 gates. A d-gate circuit computes ab and ce, adds them freely, then multiplies by the d-2 prefix variables one at a time. Hence exact multiplicative complexity is d, whereas the usual degree bound gives only d-1. This is a one-gate improvement for an explicit unbounded family, not superpolynomial demand. The same six witnesses work after adding unused spectator variables, and every coordinate in the unpadded family is essential. The proof needs neither enumerating all circuits nor choosing a target-dependent circuit as its certificate.

## 5. Exact finite replay

Run python3 research/SCC-001/budget_exterior_shadow_replay.py. It checks all nonzero homogeneous exterior symbols in degrees 2 through n for n=3,4,5 (2,165 charts, 1,764 nondecomposable certificates), comparing the determinant criterion against independently generated exterior products and verifying each certificate against every decomposable candidate. It checks the explicit six-witness family for d=2,...,7 and its constructive d-gate output by Boolean Mobius transformation. Seeded random reverse substitution from affine and nonlinear starting states on two through five inputs, at budgets zero through six, checks 2,688 outputs, 19,312 labelled product terms, and 6,144 truncation/approximation layer comparisons. Exhaustive three-bit reachable states for horizons one and two from the affine state and all nonredundant one-gate successor states give 212 maximal-shadow checks against independently enumerated initial products and 13,696 exact projected coset cuts. All passed (capture a7d7270ebe664bd3868ede9b2121e4da). This is finite author-side regression, not independent attestation or a substitute for the proofs. The one-slack exterior-rank bound is an analytic consequence of the branch theorem, not independently tested by an exhaustive rank solver.

## 6. Scope and next boundary

The maximal-layer cosets are completely classified, the intermediate-layer subspaces have simultaneous truncated atom-capacity constraints, and there is an explicit polynomial-cardinality obstruction certificate at the saturated degree horizon. These are partial budget-indexed characterizations, not a full characterization of all reachable cosets at arbitrary slack. In particular, the reverse branching dictionary forgets correlations between branches beyond their shared factor-count budget; the high-degree horizon is at most n-1. The distance and rank conditions do not provide superpolynomial lower bounds, and no claim is made that the lower-degree capacity is sufficient. The next substantive question is whether exact common-gate factor correlations among branches with large slack admit a nontrivial independent obstruction, rather than reducing to minimal atom count or the original circuit search. No independent adjudication, Lean formalization, novelty or SAT separation claim.
