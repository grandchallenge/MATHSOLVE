## 1. Separate the two support sectors before taking symbols

Fix one Boolean target f and the filtered function algebra B, F_r. Write R=sum F_r z^r, J_f={a in R:fa in R}, and P_e=R intersect B_e[z], e=0,1, where B_e is the ideal of functions supported on f=e. Then
    J_f=P_0 direct_sum P_1
as graded R-modules: a=(1+f)a+fa, and both summands belong to R exactly when a and fa do. Their supports are disjoint. The same splitting holds in every successor ring and commutes with extension of ideals since multiplication preserves supports. In degree r, (P_e)_r=(B_e intersect F_r) z^r. Do NOT identify gr J_f with a subspace of gr B by adding both sectors: leading symbols from distinct sectors may coincide even though their functions have disjoint supports. Take symbols separately.

For one charged gate g=uv, t=gz, R'=R+tR, define the relative new relation module
    Q=J'_f/(J_f R'),  Q_e=P'_e/(P_e R').
It splits as Q_0 direct_sum Q_1. Write L_r=B_e intersect F_r and L'_r=B_e intersect F'_r, using negative grades zero. The exact coefficient formula is
    (Q_e)_r = L'_r / (L_r+gL_{r-1}).
Indeed R'=R+tR implies P_e R'=P_e+tP_e. This is the kernel of the target-gluing base-change map of TARGET_GLUE.md, with the precise support-sector decomposition.

Since F'_r is contained in F_{r+1}, z P'_e is contained in P_e. Hence z annihilates Q, and t^2=zt implies t^2 Q=0. Write A=gr_F B, q=[g] in A_2 and M_e=gr L embedded in A. By the leading-symbol isomorphism of TARGET_IDEAL_TRANSPORT.md,
    (Q_e)_r is naturally isomorphic to
    ((q A_{r-1}) intersect (M_e)_{r+1}) / q(M_e)_{r-1}.
Thus Q_e as an old A-module is exactly the Tor intersection defect ((q) intersect M_e)/(q M_e), with degrees lowered by one. Old multiplication agrees with symbol multiplication by construction. However Q is an R'-module, not merely an A-module: the extra action t on Q_e is induced by multiplying an adapted representative by g. It can carry filtered information not specified by the old Tor vector spaces. More precisely, for a representative a in L'_r choose b in F_r and c in F_{r-1} with a=b+gc. Under the symbol isomorphism, t sends [a] to the class of q[b]_r in ((q A_r) intersect (M_e)_{r+2}) / q(M_e)_r: ga=gb+gc and gc is in F_{r+1}. A different decomposition changes b by an element gc' in F_r, whose product with g stays in F_r, hence gives zero in degree r+2. Independence from a modulo inherited relations follows from multiplication in Q. In particular do not infer that the minimal generator spaces over the old and new rings coincide.

Concrete nonzero t-action: use truth masks on four bits, assignment index a+2b+4c+8d. From the affine state adjoin the products of operand-mask pairs (52275,61455), (38313,22938), (30702,26985), in order (decimal). Then use (6838,50115), giving g=0x0282. All operands are available at their respective steps. For target f=0xf853 in the zero-support sector, a=0x058c lies in L'_1 but not L_1+gL_0. Its image ga=0x0080 does not belong to L_2+gL_1, so t[a] is nonzero. The profile of minimal generators is (0,1,0,0,0); it stays unchanged after adding an independent unused bit. The replay verifies the masks and the symbol action directly.

Since R' is connected graded generated in degree one, the number of minimal homogeneous new relations in degree r is
    beta_e(r)=dim L'_r / (L_r+gL_{r-1}+ V' L'_{r-1}),
where V'=F'_1 and the denominator means the span of the displayed subspaces/products. This is Q_e/R'_+ Q_e, not a count of a chosen generating list. A finite module over a connected graded algebra is generated by lifts of a basis of this quotient, by induction in degree; every generating list must span the quotient. This module is finite-dimensional because both filtrations saturate B and Q eventually vanishes. Redundant gates give Q=0.

## 2. Exact compositional accounting

For any nested rings R_0 subset R_1 subset ... subset R_t along a legal gate history, let J_i be the corresponding fixed-target relation ideals and Q_ij=J_j/(J_i R_j) for i<=j. There is a natural right-exact sequence of R_k-modules, i<=j<=k,
    Q_ij tensor_{R_j} R_k -> Q_ik -> Q_jk -> 0.
The first map is multiplication, with image J_j R_k/(J_i R_k); the second is the quotient by that image. It need not be injective, and applying the minimal generator functor (-)/R_{k,+}(-) gives another right-exact sequence, not an additive birth count. The same statements hold independently on each support sector.

More explicitly the ideals J_i R_k form a nested chain. Its successive factors are quotients of Q_{i-1,i} tensor R_k, by the same multiplication map. Their degree-wise images may overlap with products of lower-degree relations created later. There is no sum-of-minimal-generators identity. Because F^{(k)}_r is contained in F^{(i)}_{r+k-i} (iterate the one-gate inclusion), z^{k-i} Q_ik=0. The exponent records an upper bound on promotion distance, not a lower bound on total gate count for a target. No assertion that any arbitrary short relation-module filtration is realized by a legal gate history.

## 3. Exponential minimal relative births in two consecutive cheap gates

Use n=m+4 essential input bits a,b,c,d,x_1,...,x_m, m>=2, k=floor(m/2), and the single target
    f=abcd Thr_{m,k}(x).
This is monotone and every coordinate is essential. A nonzero translation period of a monotone function would force all coordinates in the translated set to be irrelevant (compare the all-zero/all-one values on that set, fixing its complement), so there are no independent affine spectators. The threshold dynamic program uses <=2mk charged multiplications, and forming the four-bit product and combining with the threshold costs four more. Thus f has a uniform O(m^2) circuit.

Start with the affine state R_0, adjoin g=ab to get R_1, then h=cd to get R_2. Both gates are legal and neither completes the target. Use the one-sector B_1, where g=h=1. Supported square-free monomials a b c d x_T with |T|>=k form a basis of B_1 by monotonicity, linear independence and counting the true inputs. Each F^{(j)}_r is spanned by ordinary square-free monomials of minimal gate-feature cover cost <=r. For the supported monomials the cost is 4+|T|-j: each of the j disjoint pair features saves exactly one factor, and no other available feature saves a factor. Expanding arbitrary products of affine combinations of the monomial features proves that no missing monomial can appear by cancellation. Intersecting two coordinate spans takes their common monomial basis.

On this support sector P_i R_j = P_i as subspaces of B[z], since each newly adjoined feature is 1 on its support and z belongs to R_i. Thus Q_ij^1 has for every supported monomial a basis class in each degree
    4+|T|-j <= r < 4+|T|-i,
and none otherwise. Products by x_l z for l not in T add the variable and increase degree by one; z shifts within the indicated interval. Products by a,b,c,d or pair gates act as z; repeat variables likewise. Consequently Q_ij^1 is minimally generated by exactly binomial(m,k) classes, all at degree 4+k-j, whenever i<j. Upper bound follows from extending k-subsets and multiplying by z; lower bound follows from linear independence in the first nonzero grade. In particular both consecutive one-gate relative modules Q_01^1 and Q_12^1 have exponentially many genuinely new minimal relations over their successor rings, despite a cheap target and no completion or spectator loophole. The full Q has at least these many generators by its sector splitting.

In the two-gate composite, the image of Q_01^1 tensor R_2 in Q_02^1 is exactly z Q_02^1, and Q_12^1 is the quotient by that image. This follows grade by grade from the two consecutive degrees for each supported monomial. The first step's entire generator image disappears in the final minimal-generator quotient, while the second step supplies the new lower-grade generators. Hence counting relative minimal births at each step double-counts the same promoted supports; even the single-step charge can be exponential on a cheap computation. This does NOT establish an exponential actual decrease for every scalar function of Q or every possible amortized cancellation scheme.

The minimal-generator profile is spectator invariant: under independent affine spectators the Rees ring and each support-sector relation module extend freely over the old Rees ring with basis spectator square-free monomials times their degree shifts. This follows from the unique coefficient expansion in spectator monomials and the filtration splitting; quotient and multiplication commute with the extension. Modding out positive-degree elements kills positive spectator degrees and retains the original minimal generators. The example is already essential in all coordinates, so removing spectators cannot repair the exponential charge.

## 4. Shared gate criterion for arbitrary prescribed families at the affine state

At the initial affine state let E be the n-dimensional space of linear input forms. The associated graded algebra A is the exterior algebra on E (over F2); square-free monomials form its basis. Let H be any family of homogeneous relation symbols of positive degree, possibly in several degrees and either support sector, with at least one nonzero symbol. Let
    D(H)={w in E: w wedge h=0 for every h in H}.
For a legal nonredundant affine gate label q=u wedge v, the entire family is promoted by one unit of filtration degree iff span(u,v) is contained in D(H).

Proof: the leading-symbol promotion criterion from section 1 is h in q A for every prescribed symbol h. In a basis extending u,v, membership in q A means every nonzero monomial contains both u and v. This is equivalent to u wedge h=v wedge h=0, since exterior multiplication by a basis vector is injective on the span of monomials omitting that vector. Both directions are invariant under a change of basis. The same argument in a basis extending D(H) shows every h contains the wedge product of a basis of D(H); in particular dim D(H) cannot exceed the least nonzero degree of H. A common nonredundant gate exists iff dim D(H)>=2. If its dimension is d, the number of distinct compatible nonzero exterior labels is ((2^d-1)(2^d-2))/6: every ordered independent pair in D gives a label, and exactly six pairs give the same two-plane and label over F2.

For the minimal true-sector threshold symbols of section 3, the common factor space is exactly span(a,b,c,d). Indeed the annihilator of each square-free basis monomial is the span of its factors, and the intersection over all k-subsets of the m threshold variables contains none of those variables, since k<m. All 35 two-plane labels in that four-space promote the entire exponential family initially. Because those symbols are in the first nonzero support grade, none is inherited via qM from a lower grade. The specific gate ab is only one of these 35 coherent choices. The criterion treats the family as a simultaneous demand, rather than the sum of separate symbol demands.

This is an exact criterion for specified initial affine-state relations, not for arbitrary nonlinear states. In a quotient of the exterior algebra the annihilation test need not imply divisibility; the actual image condition H_r subset q A_{r-1} remains necessary and sufficient for promotion of specified leading symbols in any state. In general it does not assert that all promoted symbols remain new after inherited products are removed, nor that a one-step compatible label remains compatible at later states. For example after adjoining ab on three bits a,b,c, the old grade-two symbols ac,bc,abc are independent and A_3=0. The gate ac has both operands annihilating the grade-two relation symbol bc, but bc is not in q A_0=span(ac) and does not enter the successor degree-one space. Take f=bc to realize bc as a true-sector relation. The additional filtered action from section 1 is indispensable. No necessary family for computing a general target has been established by this test.

## 6. Boundary

The exact relation-birth object now has a compositional law and an explicit obstruction to using its minimal-generator count as a polynomial gate resource, even across two legal gates before target completion. It is not sufficient to count births after quotienting out inherited relations. The missing ingredient is a coherent reuse-sensitive charge on the operation that promotes an entire family of relations, not their number or independent generator count. The marked target's remaining degree alone is bounded by n. The full module has extra obligations even after target completion (TARGET_GLUE.md); this result does not claim that quotienting them out produces superpolynomial demand. The shared-factor criterion gives an exact common promotion mechanism at the affine state. Next: extend the joint image/annihilator test with filtered correction through successive nonlinear states, then seek a nonadditive target-completing functional not reducible to support size or filtration degree. No unrestricted SAT lower bound, complexity separation or certification.
