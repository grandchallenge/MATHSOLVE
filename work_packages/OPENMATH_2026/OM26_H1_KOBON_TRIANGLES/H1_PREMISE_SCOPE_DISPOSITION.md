## Why the theorem should not be generalized here

The clean-line parity proof genuinely uses pairwise nonparallelism. For a clean line in an arrangement of even size `n`, pairwise nonparallelism gives exactly `m = n - 1` ordinary crossings, hence odd `m`. With parallel mates, the crossing count becomes `m = n - 1 - p`, where `p` is the number of parallel mates, and the parity step is not available when `p` is odd. No unrestricted-parallel version is claimed.

That restriction does not weaken the H1 upper-bound route. `H1_12_PARALLEL_REDUCTION.md` already proves that, for any finite arrangement and any finite selected collection of counted bounded triangular faces, a projective change of affine chart can eliminate every parallel pair while preserving all selected counted bounded triangular faces. Therefore a hypothetical 95-face hill witness may be normalized first to a pairwise-nonparallel affine arrangement and only then passed to the premise and prism arguments.
