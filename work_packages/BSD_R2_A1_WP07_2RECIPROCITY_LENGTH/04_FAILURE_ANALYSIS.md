# Failure analysis

## FR-01 — specialize an odd-prime ordinary BSD theorem at `p=2`

**Disposition:** terminated. Current screened theorems explicitly impose `p>2`, odd `p`, or `p not|2N`.

## FR-02 — impose `2 not| #E_tilde(F_2)` as an auxiliary hypothesis

**Disposition:** impossible in the selected class. Good ordinary at `2` forces `#E_tilde(F_2)` to be `2` or `4`.

## FR-03 — assume residual `2`-distinguishedness

**Disposition:** impossible in the standard sense for this coefficient field. Both residual ordinary characters land in `F_2^x={1}`.

## FR-04 — treat the ordinary unit-root multiplier as a 2-adic unit

**Disposition:** terminated. Under the normalization containing `(1-alpha^(-1))^2`, its exact valuation is `2` or `4`.

## FR-05 — differentiate the rank-zero interpolation identity

**Disposition:** terminated. A p-adic interpolation formula for special values does not identify the derivative of a p-adic L-function with the complex derivative `L'(E,1)`. Rank one still requires explicit reciprocity/Gross-Zagier/height or an equivalent Heegner-index theorem.

## FR-06 — use a p=2 twist-family theorem as a uniform proof

**Disposition:** quarantined to its stated family. Kriz–Li and related p=2 twist results demonstrate genuine p=2 reach but do not by their abstract statement cover every curve in the selected class.

## FR-07 — infer the missing theorem from WP06 descent control

**Disposition:** terminated. WP06 is an arithmetic comparison across quadratic base change. It contains no analytic-to-arithmetic leading-term equality.

## FR-08 — hide normalization debt inside `up to a 2-power`

**Disposition:** terminated. A power of `2` is the quantity being measured.

## Reopening rule

A route reopens only when an exact source or in-package proof meets `03_RECIPROCITY_OBLIGATION.md` under the unchanged selected hypotheses.
