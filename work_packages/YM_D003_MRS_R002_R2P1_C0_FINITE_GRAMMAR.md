## Scope

C0 is deliberately finite-cutoff and combinatorial. It does not sum an infinite expansion and does not take the ultraviolet limit. Its purpose is to define the exact type of object that later norm and summability estimates must control.

## Typed object

For fixed infrared regulator R, terminal ultraviolet depth rho, and scale i <= rho, define a two-point contribution record

`G = (V,E,ell,box,field,ct,bg,ext,F)`

with:

- finite vertex set V and finite edge/link set E;
- scale label `ell(v)` for every vertex and `ell(e)` for every propagator/decoupling link;
- box support `box(v)` in the MRS anisotropic multiscale lattice;
- field/counterterm label `field(v)`, including ordinary gauge vertices and local restoring counterterm vertices admitted by the finite-cutoff action;
- counterterm provenance `ct(v)`, distinguishing already-fixed lower-scale local insertions from the currently extracted relevant quadratic insertion;
- background label `bg(e)` for background-dependent propagators;
- exactly two distinguished external gauge-field half-edges `ext=(h_1,h_2)`;
- a subtraction forest F of proper connected two-point subgraphs.

The record is **MRS-admissible** only when every vertex, edge/link, and local counterterm label is generated by the finite-cutoff MRS expansion at the stated scales. C0 does not enlarge that source grammar.

## 1PI predicate

A connected two-point record G is 1PI when removal of any single internal propagator edge does not disconnect the two external half-edges.

This predicate is finite and decidable for every fixed G.

## Proper two-point subgraph

A proper subrecord gamma of G is a renormalization subgraph when:

1. gamma is connected and 1PI;
2. exactly two half-edges connect gamma to G\gamma;
3. gamma is proper, so gamma != G;
4. its internal scales satisfy the chosen multiscale nesting condition used by the subtraction forest.

C0 leaves the exact MRS nesting inequality as a source-concordance datum; no estimate depends on it yet.

## Local relevant projection

For every admissible two-point kernel K_G(p;B) at finite cutoff, define the quadratic relevant projection symbolically by

`P_rel K_G := [K_G]_{local,A^2/2}`.

This notation means: extract the local zero-derivative quadratic gauge-field coefficient in the normalization of MRS Eq. (III.1). It is not an invented numerical formula.

The renormalized remainder is

`R_rel K_G := K_G - P_rel K_G`.

## Constructors

The C0 class is the smallest finite class closed under the following constructors whenever the corresponding finite-cutoff MRS operation is defined:

1. `HDEC`: add one horizontal decoupling link and join the affected finite box records;
2. `VDEC`: add one vertical/anisotropic decoupling link and update scale/box labels;
3. `MAYER`: replace a finite hard-core constraint by its finite Mayer connected-graph expansion and retain a chosen connected component carrying the two external legs;
4. `SUBTRACT(gamma)`: for a proper two-point renormalization subgraph gamma in F, replace its local relevant part by the corresponding local quadratic insertion and retain its renormalized remainder in the parent amplitude;
5. `PROJECT`: apply P_rel to the outer two-point kernel;
6. `LOWER_CT`: insert an already-determined lower-scale local counterterm with provenance retained.

## Closure lemma

**Lemma C0.1 (finite grammar closure).**
Starting from any finite set of MRS-admissible primitive finite-cutoff contributions, every finite sequence of the six constructors above yields another finite typed record of the same form, with exactly two external legs whenever the constructor is applied in the two-point channel.

### Proof

Each constructor acts on finite data.

- HDEC and VDEC add finitely many labeled links/vertices and do not alter the number of distinguished external half-edges.
- MAYER expands a finite hard-core relation into a finite graph sum at fixed cutoff/finite volume; selecting a connected component containing the two external half-edges again produces a finite connected record.
- SUBTRACT(gamma) replaces a finite proper subrecord by a local insertion plus a finite remainder term while recording gamma in F; this preserves the outer two-point external interface.
- PROJECT maps a finite two-point kernel to its local quadratic coefficient and therefore preserves the declared relevant channel.
- LOWER_CT adds a finite labeled local insertion whose scale/provenance is already known.
- BGDET adds a finite determinant/normalization record already present at finite cutoff and explicitly preserves the exceptional large-field/background association and its uncancelled local counterterm interface; it does not convert that sector into an ordinary vertical-expansion polymer.

Thus the structural record type is closed under every finite composition of the constructors. QED.

## What C0.1 does not prove

C0.1 is a grammar theorem, not a convergence theorem. It does not prove:

- that the source paper's informal two-point/Mayer language is fully represented by these constructors;
- absolute convergence or summability of any graph/polymer series;
- existence of a useful Banach norm;
- a scale gain after R_rel;
- uniformity in rho;
- bounded response to the mass parameter;
- exact mass tuning.

Those are C1-C5.
