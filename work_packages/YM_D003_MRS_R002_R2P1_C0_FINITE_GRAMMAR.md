## Typed finite-cutoff object

For fixed infrared regulator R, terminal ultraviolet depth rho, and scale i <= rho, a two-point contribution is a finite record

`G = (V,E,ell,box,field,ct,bg,ext,F)`

carrying finite vertices/links, scale and box labels, source-admitted field/counterterm labels, background-dependent propagator labels, exactly two external gauge-field half-edges, and a subtraction forest of proper connected 1PI two-point subgraphs.

The record is MRS-admissible only when every primitive label is generated by the finite-cutoff MRS expansion.

## Two distinct local projections

The C3 replay corrects an ambiguity in the original C0 grammar.

For a translation-invariant finite-cutoff two-point kernel K:

- `P_mass K` extracts only the local zero-derivative `A^2/2` coefficient. This is the exact mass channel.
- `P_2,loc K` extracts the complete source-admitted quadratic local Taylor jet through superficial degree two: `A^2/2`, `A(-Delta)A`, and `(dA)^2`.

The renormalized irrelevant remainder is

`R_2 K = (1-P_2,loc)K`.

Mass tuning uses `P_mass`; nested two-point renormalization uses `P_2,loc`.

## Constructors

The class is the smallest finite class closed under:

1. `HDEC`: horizontal decoupling links;
2. `VDEC`: vertical/anisotropic decoupling links;
3. `MAYER`: finite Mayer removal of hard-core constraints in the two-point connected component;
4. `SUBTRACT(gamma)`: replace the complete local quadratic jet `P_2,loc K_gamma` of a proper two-point renormalization subgraph by its source-admitted local insertion and retain `R_2 K_gamma`;
5. `PROJECT_MASS`: extract `P_mass`;
6. `PROJECT_2LOC`: extract `P_2,loc`;
7. `LOWER_CT`: insert already-determined lower-scale local counterterms with provenance;
8. `BGDET`: attach the finite background-determinant/normalization record directly to its large-field region, preserving the Section VI exceptional-sector interface rather than treating it as an ordinary vertical-expansion polymer.

## Closure lemma

Each constructor acts on finite data and preserves the two-point external interface when used in the two-point channel. HDEC/VDEC add finite labeled links, MAYER produces a finite connected graph sum at finite cutoff, SUBTRACT replaces a proper finite subrecord by a finite source-admitted local insertion plus remainder, the two projections map a finite kernel to finite local coefficients, LOWER_CT adds a finite labeled insertion, and BGDET adds a finite region-local determinant record.

Therefore every finite composition of these constructors is again a finite typed C0 record.
