## 3. The residue field at `(2)`

The height-one localization is

`A=Lambda_(2)`.

Because `T notin (2)`, `T` is a unit in `A`. Therefore

`A/2A ~= Frac(F_2[[T]]) = F_2((T))`.

This point is decisive. The residue field at `(2)` is not `F_2`.

Consequently a factor `T^r` is invisible to the height-one `(2)` valuation: it is a unit in `A`, even though it is a nonunit in every finite local group ring `F_2[Gamma_n]`.

### Lemma `BSD-R5-PRIM-RESIDUE-002`

For `f in Lambda`, the following are equivalent:

1. `f` is a unit in `A`;
2. `f notin 2A`;
3. the image of `f` in `F_2((T))` is nonzero;
4. the reduction `bar f in F_2[[T]]` is nonzero.

### Proof

`A` is a DVR with maximal ideal generated by `2`, so (1) and (2) are equivalent. Reduction gives (2) iff the residue is nonzero in `A/2A=F_2((T))`. The natural map `F_2[[T]] -> F_2((T))` is injective because `F_2[[T]]` is a domain, giving (3) iff (4). QED.

## 5. Finite cyclotomic detection

At `m=1`,

`R_{1,n}=F_2[Gamma_n]`.

If `Gamma_n` is generated by `gamma` and `T=gamma-1`, then in characteristic two

`(1+T)^(2^n)-1 = T^(2^n)`.

Thus

`R_{1,n} ~= F_2[[T]]/(T^(2^n))`.

The compatible rank-one line satisfies

`KS_Lambda/2 ~= lim_n KS_1(T_{1,n})`,

with the transition maps induced by the quotient

`F_2[[T]]/(T^(2^(n+1))) -> F_2[[T]]/(T^(2^n))`.

The module `KS_Lambda/2` is free rank one over the `T`-adically separated ring `F_2[[T]]`. Hence

`intersection_n T^(2^n)(KS_Lambda/2)=0`.

Therefore an element of `KS_Lambda/2` is nonzero if and only if its image is nonzero in at least one finite quotient.

### Theorem `BSD-R5-PRIM-FINITE-DETECT-004`

The following are equivalent:

1. `R5-PRIM`;
2. `bar kappa_Lambda^{Kato} != 0` in `KS_Lambda/2`;
3. there exists `n>=0` such that
   `kappa_{1,n}^{Kato} != 0` in `KS_1(T_{1,n})`.

The finite-layer condition is **nonvanishing**, not basis status.

### Proof

(1)<=> (2) is Theorem `RESIDUAL-003`. The inverse-limit description above identifies the reduction of `kappa_Lambda^{Kato}` with the compatible family `(kappa_{1,n}^{Kato})_n`. T-adic separatedness gives (2)<=> (3). QED.
