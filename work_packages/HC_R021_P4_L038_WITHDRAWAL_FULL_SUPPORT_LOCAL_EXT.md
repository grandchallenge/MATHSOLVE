## 1. Purpose

Correct `HC-R021-L038` before any further route pruning.

`L038` attempted to transfer the characteristic-foliation/Fitting-locus argument used for modules supported on a hypersurface (`L022`, `L030`) to the full-support ideal module which occurs generically in Markman's partial-normalization secant object.

That transfer is not valid. At a generic point of a smooth codimension-two `W_2` component, the full-support ideal is a perfect module of projective dimension one. Its local self-`Ext^2` vanishes. Hence every local degree-two Hochschild obstruction class vanishes on a sufficiently small affine neighborhood. The nonconstant Gauss map of `W_2` therefore cannot prove nonvanishing of the global object obstruction.

Accordingly:

```text
HC-R021-L038 is withdrawn and superseded by this package.
```

The partial-normalization/full-support route is reopened.

## 2. Generic local model

Retain the valid source reduction from `L038`.

At a generic point of one `W_2` component, away from all pairwise intersections and normalization points, Markman's object

```text
F'=[O_X -> nu_* O_(Z_tilde)]
```

restricts to

```text
[ R -> R/I ] ~= I,
```

where

```text
R
```

is a regular local ring of dimension four and

```text
I=(f,g)
```

is generated by a regular sequence defining the smooth codimension-two surface.

There is a free resolution

```text
0 -> R --(-g,f)^T--> R^2 --(f,g)--> I -> 0.   (2.1)
```

Thus

```text
pd_R(I)=1.                                    (2.2)
```

## 3. Local self-Ext

Apply `Hom_R(-,I)` to (2.1). The resulting complex is

```text
I^2 --(-g,f)--> I.
```

Its degree-one cokernel is

```text
Ext^1_R(I,I)
 ~= I/(fI+gI)
 = I/I^2,                                    (3.1)
```

while there are no higher terms. Therefore

```text
Ext^j_R(I,I)=0,  j>=2.                       (3.2)
```

In particular

```text
Ext^2_R(I,I)=0.                              (3.3)
```

### HC-R021-L039a — generic local obstruction vanishing

For every ambient first-order Hochschild class `xi`, the restriction of the characteristic obstruction

```text
ob_I(xi) in Ext^2_R(I,I)
```

to this affine local model is zero.

This does not imply that the global obstruction of `F'` vanishes. It implies only that any such global obstruction is a gluing/local-to-global class rather than a pointwise projective-dimension-two obstruction.

## 4. Why the L038 Fitting argument does not apply

The characteristic-line argument used in `L022` and `L030` concerns a coherent module whose ambient annihilator cuts out a coisotropic hypersurface. A lift over a Poisson deformation then induces the familiar partial connection along the characteristic distribution on that support, and Fitting ideals of the module *on the support* are preserved.

The generic module in the present case is different:

```text
I subset R
```

has full ambient support and zero annihilator. A first-order lift as a left module over a noncommutative deformation of `R` does not, merely from its existence, furnish the ordinary derivation connection on `I` assumed in `L038` strongly enough to force the commutative Fitting ideal `(f,g)` to be invariant under the entire Poisson image distribution.

Equation (3.3) gives a decisive consistency check: such a claimed local obstruction cannot be nonzero because the local obstruction group itself is zero.

Therefore the implication

```text
ob_I(pi)=0  =>  im(pi^sharp) subset T_S
```

asserted for this full-support ideal in `L038` is unsupported and in general cannot be used here.

## 5. Local-to-global location of the true obstruction

The local self-Ext sheaves of a smooth codimension-two ideal have

```text
mathcal Hom(I,I) ~= O_X,
mathcal Ext^1(I,I) ~= I/I^2,
mathcal Ext^j(I,I)=0 for j>=2.              (5.1)
```

Consequently the local-to-global spectral sequence places a global class in

```text
Ext^2_X(I,I)
```

in filtrations arising from

```text
H^2(X,O_X)
```

and

```text
H^1(S,I/I^2),                               (5.2)
```

up to the usual extension/differential data.

This is exactly the regime in which same-support global cup products and derived-dual couplings can matter. The generic local model does not prune them.

## 6. Derived dual of the generic ideal

Let

```text
D:=RHom_R(I,R).
```

Dualizing (2.1) represents `D` by

```text
R^2 --(-g,f)--> R
```

in cohomological degrees `0,1`. Hence

```text
H^0(D) ~= R,
H^1(D) ~= R/I.                              (6.1)
```

Equivalently, there is a distinguished triangle

```text
R -> D -> (R/I)[-1] -> R[1].               (6.2)
```

This is the first genuinely different same-support partner encountered in the campaign: it contains both a full-support rank-one term and a surface-supported correction.

Furthermore, since `I` is perfect,

```text
RHom_R(D,I)
 ~= I tensor^L I.                           (6.3)
```

Tensoring (2.1) with `I` shows that

```text
Tor^R_1(I,I)=0,                             (6.4)
```

because `I -> I^2`, `h |-> (-gh,fh)`, is injective. Therefore

```text
RHom_R(D,I)
```

is concentrated in degree zero locally.

In the opposite direction,

```text
RHom_R(I,D)
 ~= D tensor^L D,                           (6.5)
```

and the length-two free resolution `P tensor P` gives

```text
Ext^2_R(I,D) ~= R/I.                        (6.6)
```

Thus after shifting `D` by one, a pair of opposite degree-one channels exists locally:

```text
Ext^1_R(D[1],I)=Hom_R(D,I),
Ext^1_R(I,D[1])=Ext^2_R(I,D).               (6.7)
```

Their product into the local diagonal group of `I` is nevertheless zero because

```text
Ext^2_R(I,I)=0.                             (6.8)
```

This is not a no-go: the actual global obstruction is already forced into the local-to-global filtrations (5.2), where global products can be nonzero.

## 7. HC-R021-L039 — corrected disposition

### Statement

For the generic codimension-two component of Markman's partial-normalization/full-support secant object:

1. the local model is the perfect ideal `I=(f,g)` of projective dimension one;
2. `Ext^2_R(I,I)=0`, so no generic pointwise mixed obstruction can be inferred from the `W_2` Gauss map;
3. the Fitting-invariance argument of `L038` is not a valid obstruction test for this full-support left module;
4. the global obstruction, if nonzero, lies in local-to-global filtration generated by `H^2(O_X)` and `H^1(I/I^2)`;
5. the derived dual supplies a nontrivial same-support partner with a surface-supported degree-one cohomology sheaf and opposite local channels after shift.

Therefore `L038` is withdrawn. The partial-normalization and derived-dual lanes are live.

### Proof

Sections 2-6.

QED.

## 8. Effect on earlier results

This correction does **not** withdraw `L022` or `L030`. Their characteristic-foliation arguments are applied to modules supported on a hypersurface, where the partial connection is along the characteristic foliation of that support. The invalid step in `L038` was extending that support argument to a full-support ideal module.

It also does not assert that Markman's `F'` is semiregular or that either mixed obstruction vanishes globally.
