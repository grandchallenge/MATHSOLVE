# WP31 theorem — finite-layer evaluation of the toroidal formal norm class

## 1. Protected setup

Let `E/Q` lie in the protected selected `BSD-R2-A1` branch, and let `P` be a saturated generator of

`E(Q)/E(Q)_tors`.

Protected WP28 gives the finite formal universal-norm quotient

`F_2^norm`

and its distinguished class

`z_form(P) in F_2^norm`.

Protected WP29 defines

`m_2:=ord_2(3-a_2) in {1,2}`

and the canonical depth

`tau_form(P) in {0,...,m_2}`,

with

`ord(z_form(P))=2^{m_2-tau_form(P)}`.

Protected WP30 identifies Hall/Tan's one-dimensional twist scalar with the protected ordinary unit root:

`u=alpha`.

Therefore

`ord_2(1-alpha)=m_2`

and

`(1-alpha)Z_2=(1-alpha^(-1))Z_2`.

The protected MATHFORGE Hall source audit admits the finite-layer toroidal isomorphism and the explicit commutative diagram used to construct it.

## 2. Finite cyclotomic layers

For `n>=1`, let

`L_n/Q_2`

be the degree-`2^n` layer of the local cyclotomic `Z_2`-extension and put

`G_n:=Gal(L_n/Q_2) ~= Z/2^n Z`.

Define the finite formal norm quotient

`F_{2,n}^norm
 := Ehat(2Z_2)/N_{L_n/Q_2}(Ehat(O_{L_n}))`.

Here `Ehat` denotes the formal group of `E` at `2`, with the same protected formal-group normalization used in WP28.

The inclusion

`N_2^infinity(Ehat) subset N_{L_n/Q_2}(Ehat(O_{L_n}))`

induces a natural surjection

`q_n:F_2^norm -> F_{2,n}^norm`.

### Theorem `BSD-A1-WP31-FINITE-SIZE-001`

For every `n>=1`,

`#F_{2,n}^norm = 2^{min(n,m_2)}`.

### Proof

Hall's admitted finite-layer theorem, specialized to dimension one and composed with protected WP30, gives

`F_{2,n}^norm ~= G_n/(1-alpha)G_n`.

Choose a generator of `G_n`, identifying it with `Z/2^n Z`. Multiplication by `1-alpha` has `2`-adic valuation `m_2`, so its image is the subgroup generated by `2^{min(n,m_2)}` up to multiplication by an odd unit. Hence the cokernel has order

`2^{min(n,m_2)}`.

QED.

### Corollary `BSD-A1-WP31-STABILIZATION-001`

For every `n>=m_2`, the natural map

`q_n:F_2^norm -> F_{2,n}^norm`

is an isomorphism.

In particular the layer `n=m_2` determines every element of the infinite formal universal-norm quotient.

### Proof

Protected WP28 gives

`#F_2^norm=2^{m_2}`.

The preceding theorem gives the same order for `F_{2,n}^norm` when `n>=m_2`. The map `q_n` is surjective, hence a surjection between finite groups of equal order. It is therefore an isomorphism.

QED.

Thus the infinite-tower problem stabilizes in a quadratic layer if `a_2=+1` and in a quartic layer if `a_2=-1`.

## 3. Hall's finite-layer square

Fix `n>=m_2`.

Let `T/Q_2` denote Hall's completed maximal unramified `2`-extension and put

`E_n:=L_n T`.

After choosing a source-compatible toroidal isomorphism `k`, the protected Hall construction identifies formal points with the twisted principal-unit module

`V_alpha`.

Let

`v(P):=k(z_form(P)) in V_alpha(Q_2)`

represent the finite-layer image of the protected formal class.

Hall's finite-layer proof supplies an exact row

`1 -> G_n --i_n--> U^1(E_n)/I_{G_n}U^1(E_n) --N--> U^1(T) -> 1`

and a commutative vertical square whose maps are

- multiplication by `1-alpha` on `G_n`;
- `phi-alpha` on the principal-unit coinvariant group;
- `phi-alpha` on `U^1(T)`.

For a fixed prime element `pi` of `L_n`, Hall writes the injection explicitly as

`i_n(g)=[pi^{g-1}]`.

The kernel of `phi-alpha` on the bottom principal-unit term is exactly `V_alpha(Q_2)`.

## 4. Elementwise connecting-map formula

### Construction `BSD-A1-WP31-GALOIS-LIFT-001`

Choose any

`y in U^1(E_n)/I_{G_n}U^1(E_n)`

such that

`N(y)=v(P)`.

Such a lift exists by exactness of Hall's row.

Because `v(P)` lies in `V_alpha(Q_2)`,

`(phi-alpha)v(P)=1`.

By commutativity with norm,

`N((phi-alpha)y)
 =(phi-alpha)N(y)
 =(phi-alpha)v(P)
 =1`.

Hence

`(phi-alpha)y in ker(N)=im(i_n)`.

Since `i_n` is injective, there is a unique element

`g_n(P;y) in G_n`

such that

`i_n(g_n(P;y))=(phi-alpha)y`.

### Lemma `BSD-A1-WP31-LIFT-INVARIANCE-001`

The class

`[g_n(P;y)] in G_n/(1-alpha)G_n`

is independent of the chosen norm lift `y`.

### Proof

Let `y'` be another lift. Then

`N(y'/y)=1`,

so exactness gives a unique `h in G_n` such that

`y'/y=i_n(h)`.

Apply `phi-alpha`. By commutativity of Hall's square,

`(phi-alpha)i_n(h)=i_n((1-alpha)h)`.

Therefore

`i_n(g_n(P;y'))
 =i_n(g_n(P;y)) i_n((1-alpha)h)`.

Injectivity of `i_n` gives

`g_n(P;y')=g_n(P;y)+(1-alpha)h`

in additive notation on `G_n`.

Thus their classes modulo `(1-alpha)G_n` agree.

QED.

### Definition `BSD-A1-WP31-TWREC-001`

Define the finite twisted-reciprocity class

`twrec_n(P)
 :=[g_n(P;y)]
 in G_n/(1-alpha)G_n`.

The preceding lemma makes this independent of `y`.

### Theorem `BSD-A1-WP31-CONNECTING-001`

Under Hall's finite-layer toroidal norm isomorphism, `twrec_n(P)` is the image of the finite formal norm class `q_n(z_form(P))`, up to the conventional inversion/sign choice for the Snake-Lemma connecting morphism.

Consequently

`ord(twrec_n(P))
 =ord(q_n(z_form(P)))`.

For `n>=m_2`, this equals

`ord(z_form(P))`.

### Proof

Hall constructs the isomorphism

`V_alpha(Q_2)/N_{L_n/Q_2}V_alpha(L_n)
 -> G_n/(1-alpha)G_n`

by applying the Snake Lemma to precisely the commutative square used above.

The elementwise definition of the Snake connecting morphism is: lift an element in the kernel of the lower vertical map through the surjective horizontal map, apply the upper vertical map, identify the result in the left kernel, and take its cokernel class. This is exactly the construction of `g_n(P;y)` above. Different conventions for multiplicative versus additive connecting maps can invert the resulting quotient element, but inversion is a group automorphism and preserves element order.

The toroidal map `k` identifies the formal norm class with the corresponding `V_alpha` class. The final assertion follows from Corollary `BSD-A1-WP31-STABILIZATION-001`.

QED.

## 5. Stabilization at degree at most four

Set

`n_0:=m_2`.

Then

`G_{n_0} ~= Z/2^{m_2}Z`

and

`twrec_{n_0}(P)`

already determines the infinite-tower class.

Choose a generator `sigma` of `G_{n_0}`. Write

`g_{n_0}(P;y)=sigma^{c_y(P)}`

with

`c_y(P) in Z/2^{m_2}Z`.

Changing the norm lift changes `c_y(P)` by a multiple of `1-alpha`; since

`ord_2(1-alpha)=m_2`,

this changes the integer exponent by a multiple of `2^{m_2}`. Thus its residue class modulo `2^{m_2}` is independent of the lift.

Changing the generator `sigma` multiplies the exponent by an odd unit, so its truncated `2`-adic valuation is unchanged.

### Definition `BSD-A1-WP31-EXPONENT-001`

Let

`c_tw(P) in Z/2^{m_2}Z`

be the finite twisted-reciprocity exponent, defined up to multiplication by an odd unit.

Define

`tau_tw(P):=min(m_2,ord_2(c_tw(P)))`,

with the zero residue assigned depth `m_2`.

### Theorem `BSD-A1-WP31-TAU-001`

One has the exact identity

`tau_form(P)=tau_tw(P)`.

Hence

`tau_form(P)
 =min(m_2,ord_2(c_tw(P)))`.

### Proof

By Theorem `BSD-A1-WP31-CONNECTING-001` and stabilization,

`twrec_{m_2}(P)`

has the same element order as `z_form(P)`.

In the cyclic group `Z/2^{m_2}Z`, an element represented by `c` has additive order

`2^{m_2-min(m_2,ord_2(c))}`.

Protected WP29 defines `tau_form(P)` uniquely by

`ord(z_form(P))=2^{m_2-tau_form(P)}`.

Comparing the two formulas gives

`tau_form(P)=min(m_2,ord_2(c_tw(P)))`.

QED.

## 6. Complete finite classification

### Case `a_2=+1`

Then `m_2=1` and only the quadratic local layer `L_1/Q_2` is required.

The exponent lies in `Z/2Z`:

- `c_tw(P)=1 mod 2` iff `tau_form(P)=0`;
- `c_tw(P)=0 mod 2` iff `tau_form(P)=1`.

### Case `a_2=-1`

Then `m_2=2` and only the quartic local layer `L_2/Q_2` is required.

The exponent lies in `Z/4Z`:

- `c_tw(P)` odd iff `tau_form(P)=0`;
- `c_tw(P)=2 mod 4` iff `tau_form(P)=1`;
- `c_tw(P)=0 mod 4` iff `tau_form(P)=2`.

Thus every possible value of the remaining local depth is detected by one finite twisted-reciprocity exponent in a degree-`2` or degree-`4` extension.

## 8. What has actually been reduced

WP31 does not evaluate `c_tw(P)` uniformly.

It proves something narrower but concrete:

- no infinite inverse-limit computation is required for the remaining formal coordinate;
- no level above `2` is required;
- the exact datum is a single exponent modulo `2` or `4`;
- that exponent is obtained by Hall's explicit norm-lift, `phi-alpha`, and `pi^{g-1}` procedure.

Thus the active D1b boundary is refined from

`MISSING_P2_TOROIDAL_FORMAL_COORDINATE_CONGRUENCE_DEPTH`

to

`MISSING_P2_FINITE_TWISTED_RECIPROCITY_EXPONENT`.

A successor must either evaluate this finite exponent from the local coordinates of the saturated generator, or prove an exact comparison with another protected integral invariant that determines it.
