## Why this tranche

The protected route state says:

- Balaban supplies four-dimensional large-field control and ultraviolet stability but the equation-level successor is externally source-byte blocked.
- MRS supplies a qualified source-stated ultraviolet-cutoff-free Schwinger construction for pure `SU(2)`, trivial topological sector, at fixed infrared cutoff.
- MRS explicitly does not remove the infrared cutoff, does not prove the complete OS profile, and does not treat nontrivial topology.
- The protected source audit also records a proof-completeness qualification: the authors do not provide every detailed convergence proof.

The next useful task is therefore not another general abstract theorem and not another broad source search. It is to convert the single opaque label "proof-completeness limitation" into a source-bound theorem dependency graph and close the smallest material missing proof node that can be closed.
