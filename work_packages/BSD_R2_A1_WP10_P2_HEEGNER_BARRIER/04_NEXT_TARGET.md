# Next analytic target

## `BSD-R2-A1-2PRIMARY-DIRECT-LENGTH`

WP10 has shown that the standard odd-prime Heegner-primitivity proof technology does not mechanically extend to `p=2`, and that an auxiliary-`K` index formula alone would still leave an exact rank-zero twist contribution and explicit quadratic-descent defects.

The preferred next target is therefore a direct integral theorem over `Q`.

### Target theorem shape

Under the protected `BSD-R2-A1` hypotheses, prove an exact identity that determines

`len_Z2 Sha(E/Q)[2^infinity] + sum_{ell|N} ord_2(c_ell)`

directly from the WP00-normalized complex leading term

`ord_2(L'(E,1)/(Omega_E Reg_E))`.

WP05 has already proved that the rational torsion term has zero `ord_2`, so no torsion correction remains.

The theorem may be formulated through a Heegner/Kolyvagin index, an exact Selmer length, an integral Euler-system relation, or another arithmetic object, but every 2-primary correction must be explicit. A statement only after inverting `2`, only up to a `2`-adic unit, or only up to an unspecified power of `2` is insufficient.

### Residual-image coverage

WP10 proves that irreducible `E[2]` has residual image either `C_3` or `S_3 ~= GL_2(F_2)`. A theorem intended to settle the selected class must therefore cover both image branches, or prove separate exact theorems whose union covers both.

The admitted Chao Li obstruction is confined to a stronger surjective (`S_3`) subcase with additional local hypotheses. It must not be silently extended to the `C_3` branch.

### Auxiliary-`K` alternative

An auxiliary-field route remains admissible only if it supplies, in one exact composition:

1. a p=2 integral Heegner/Kolyvagin primitivity or sharp index theorem over the WP09 field `K`;
2. the exact rank-zero twist contribution for `E^D/Q`;
3. every surviving WP06 finite descent defect;
4. the complete WP00 period, regulator, Tamagawa, isogeny, Manin, Euler, and local-normalization reconciliation.

The standard odd-prime rank-lowering/primitivity argument may not be reused at `p=2` without a genuinely new proof of the missing integral step.

### Iwasawa alternative

The Iwasawa lane remains independent. It still requires:

- exact height-one `(2)` / relative-mu control from WP08;
- exact p-adic-height and p-adic-L-function normalization comparison to the WP00 complex leading term after WP09.

It should not be substituted for the direct arithmetic target unless it closes both debts.

## Success criterion

The next tranche succeeds only if it proves, or admits and correctly composes, an exact theorem sufficient to determine the selected `ord_2` valuation without hidden powers of `2` and without narrowing the selected curve class silently.

## Stop rule

If no admitted theorem supplies such an integral p=2 direct-length bridge, and no in-package proof establishes it, the campaign has reached a genuine new-mathematics frontier. Further progress then requires a new p=2 arithmetic theorem or discovery and admission of a theorem not yet present in the governed source set.
