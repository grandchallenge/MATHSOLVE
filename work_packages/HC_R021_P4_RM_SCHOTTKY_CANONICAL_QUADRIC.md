## 1. Purpose

Resolve `RM-SCHOTTKY-A1` from `L042` to an exact canonical-curve linear-algebra test.

The six ordinary target directions `k_1,...,k_6` form the tangent space to the selected real-multiplication deformation locus. The genus-four Jacobian locus is a hypersurface in the ppav moduli space. Whether the one-dimensional Schottky defect of `L041-L042` meets the six-dimensional target kernel is therefore an incidence question between a six-plane and a hyperplane.

At a non-hyperelliptic genus-four Jacobian this incidence is controlled by the unique quadric containing the canonical curve.

## 2. RM eigenspace decomposition

Let

```text
V:=H^0(C,K_C),
dim V=4.
```

The real quadratic field `F` acts on `V`. Over `C` the two real embeddings give a decomposition

```text
V=E_1 direct_sum E_2,
dim E_1=dim E_2=2.                           (2.1)
```

The cotangent space to `A_4` at the principally polarized Jacobian is

```text
T^*_(J(C)) A_4 ~= Sym^2 V.                 (2.2)
```

The tangent space preserving the `F`-action has dimension six and, under the dual decomposition, is

```text
T_RM
 ~= Sym^2(E_1^vee) direct_sum Sym^2(E_2^vee).   (2.3)
```

Its annihilator in `Sym^2 V` is exactly the mixed block

```text
Ann(T_RM)=E_1 tensor E_2,                  (2.4)
```

of dimension four.

By `L013`, after identifying the campaign's normal form with the RM eigensplitting,

```text
T_RM=span(k_1,...,k_6)                     (2.5)
```

inside the commutative Hochschild sector.

## 3. Infinitesimal Torelli in genus four

For a non-hyperelliptic genus-four curve, the cotangent map of Torelli is multiplication of canonical forms

```text
mu:Sym^2 V -> H^0(C,2K_C).                 (3.1)
```

The dimensions are

```text
dim Sym^2 V=10,
dim H^0(2K_C)=9.
```

Infinitesimal Torelli makes `mu` surjective. Therefore

```text
ker(mu)=<Q_C>                              (3.2)
```

is one-dimensional, generated by the unique canonical quadric

```text
Q_C subset P(V^vee)
```

containing the canonical model of `C`.

Consequently the Jacobian tangent hyperplane is

```text
T_Jac = Ann(Q_C) subset T_(J(C)) A_4.      (3.3)
```

## 4. Exact incidence criterion

Combining (2.4) and (3.3),

```text
T_RM subset T_Jac
```

if and only if `Q_C` annihilates every vector in `T_RM`, equivalently

```text
boxed[ Q_C in E_1 tensor E_2 ].            (4.1)
```

Write the canonical quadratic form according to

```text
Sym^2 V
 = Sym^2 E_1
   direct_sum (E_1 tensor E_2)
   direct_sum Sym^2 E_2
```

as

```text
Q_C=Q_11+Q_12+Q_22.                        (4.2)
```

Then (4.1) is exactly

```text
Q_11=0,
Q_22=0.                                    (4.3)
```

Thus all six ordinary target directions are Jacobian directions if and only if the canonical quadric is purely mixed with respect to the two RM eigenspaces.

### HC-R021-L043a — canonical-quadric criterion

```text
span(k_1,...,k_6) subset T_Jac
iff
Q_C is purely E_1-E_2 mixed.
```

This is an exact source-specific algebraic test.

## 5. The transverse case

Suppose

```text
Q_C notin E_1 tensor E_2.                  (5.1)
```

Then the nonzero linear functional defined by `Q_C` restricts nontrivially to the six-dimensional `T_RM`. Therefore

```text
dim(T_RM intersect T_Jac)=5.              (5.2)
```

There is a one-dimensional quotient

```text
T_RM/(T_RM intersect T_Jac)                (5.3)
```

of RM-preserving ppav directions transverse to the Jacobian locus.

By `L041-L042`, every nonzero vector in this quotient gives a nonzero object obstruction for a standard genus-four `W_2`-secant block, although its Chern-character contraction vanishes.

Hence in the transverse case a standard genus-four secant block fails at least one nonzero linear combination of the six ordinary target relations.

No preferred basis vector `k_i` need be the failing direction; the invariant statement is the one-dimensional quotient (5.3).

## 6. The exceptional mixed-quadric case

Suppose instead

```text
Q_C in E_1 tensor E_2.                     (6.1)
```

Then

```text
T_RM subset T_Jac.                         (6.2)
```

The Schottky-normal obstruction line of `L041-L042` is transverse to the entire six-dimensional target ordinary kernel. Therefore the existence of the Schottky defect on each individual secant object does not, by itself, obstruct any of `k_1,...,k_6`.

This does not prove that the six object obstructions vanish; it only removes the one-dimensional Schottky argument as a reason they must fail.

## 7. Geometric form of the exceptional condition

Choose homogeneous coordinates adapted to (2.1):

```text
[x_1:x_2] on E_1^vee,
[y_1:y_2] on E_2^vee.
```

If (6.1) holds, the canonical quadric has equation

```text
x^T B y=0                                  (7.1)
```

for a `2x2` matrix `B`. In particular it contains the two projective eigenlines

```text
P(E_1^vee),
P(E_2^vee)                                 (7.2)
```

as lines on the quadric.

For a smooth genus-four canonical quadric, these are lines belonging to its rulings. Thus the exceptional incidence has a concrete projective-geometric signature: both RM eigenspaces determine lines lying on the unique canonical quadric.

This form is useful for an explicit source curve or for a familywise algebraic test.

## 8. Algebraicity and genericity of the condition

Within any algebraic family of non-hyperelliptic genus-four curves carrying the fixed RM Hodge decomposition, the components

```text
Q_11,
Q_22
```

vary algebraically. Therefore the exceptional condition

```text
Q_11=Q_22=0                                (8.1)
```

is Zariski closed on the locus where the eigenspace splitting and unique canonical quadric are defined.

Consequently, to prove that a Hodge-generic admissible source can be chosen in the transverse case it suffices to exhibit **one** admissible RM Jacobian for which either pure component is nonzero. Conversely, if the source RM-Jacobian locus is contained in (8.1), that containment is an additional structural property which must be proved rather than assumed.

The present campaign source does not specify an explicit canonical model from which (8.1) can be evaluated immediately.

## 9. HC-R021-L043 — exact resolution of RM-SCHOTTKY-A1 to a source test

### Statement

Let `C` be the non-hyperelliptic genus-four source curve with real quadratic multiplication and eigenspace splitting `H^0(K_C)=E_1 direct_sum E_2`. Let `Q_C` be its unique canonical quadric. Then

```text
span(k_1,...,k_6) subset T_Jac
iff
Q_C in E_1 tensor E_2.                    (9.1)
```

If the right-hand condition fails, the simultaneous `(D,H)`-preserving tangent meets the Jacobian tangent in dimension exactly five and standard genus-four secant blocks have a nonzero ordinary target obstruction direction by `L041-L042`.

If the right-hand condition holds, the Schottky defect lies outside the six ordinary target directions and another calculation is needed.

### Proof

Sections 2-6.

QED.
