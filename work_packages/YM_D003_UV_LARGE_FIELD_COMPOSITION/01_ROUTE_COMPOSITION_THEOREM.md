# YM-D003-R001-L001 — route-separated composition theorem

## Statement

Let T_B denote protected theorem interface YM-T-210 and T_M protected interface YM-T-220.

The current protected records imply all of the following.

### A. Balaban-route closure

Within the source Balaban lattice-RG framework, T_B supplies a theorem interface for:

1. four-dimensional large-field control by the R operation;
2. four-dimensional ultraviolet stability.

Accordingly, 'large-field control absent' is no longer a valid D003 blocker for this route.

### B. MRS-route qualified UV interface

Within the source MRS construction, T_M supplies only a qualified interface for UV-cutoff removal of the source Schwinger functions at fixed infrared cutoff, in pure SU(2), trivial topological sector, with the proof-completeness and OS limitations recorded by Programme.

### C. Cross-route noncomposition

The protected records do not supply a theorem identifying the regulated theories, observables, effective actions, Schwinger functions, or limiting objects strongly enough to transfer theorem content from one route to the other.

Therefore T_B and T_M are parallel bounded inputs, not sequential premises.

### D. Residual D003 debt

It is invalid to conclude from T_B and T_M alone that only infrared removal remains.

The protected residual obligations include:

- on the Balaban route: an actual continuum-observable existence theorem from UV stability, uniqueness/full identification, and IR/infinite-volume removal;
- on the MRS route: proof-completeness closure if theorem-grade downstream use is required, IR/infinite-volume removal, and full OS/topological/physical identification;
- on either route: the cross-debt OS, physical-scale, observable, and spectral obligations required by D002/D001/D004/D005.

## Proof

The proof is by exact typing of the protected interfaces.

### Step 1 — type the Balaban interface

YM-T-210 has regulator type 'lattice renormalization group with ultraviolet refinement', source finite-volume lattice scope, and direction 'large-field control and ultraviolet stability'. Its protected conclusion is that the large-field R-operation closes the large-field obstruction and completes the source ultraviolet-stability proof.

Its explicit claim boundary excludes IR/infinite-volume removal, uniqueness of a full continuum limit, complete OS reconstruction, physical scale setting, and mass gap.

Thus the Balaban interface closes the D003 large-field/UV-stability sub-obligation, but no stronger conclusion is licensed.

### Step 2 — type the MRS interface

YM-T-220 has regulator type 'UV cutoff removed with fixed IR cutoff retained', gauge group SU(2), trivial topological sector, and direction 'UV-cutoff removal at fixed IR'. Its source-stated conclusion is the UV limit of its Schwinger functions and Slavnov identities.

Its explicit claim boundary records incomplete detailed convergence proofs, no IR removal, no complete OS profile, and no nontrivial topology.

Thus it is a qualified fixed-IR construction interface, not a terminal continuum theorem.

### Step 3 — test sequential composition

Sequential theorem composition requires the conclusion object of the first theorem to meet the input contract of the second, or an admitted bridge theorem must provide the identification.

The protected Programme supplement records no bridge connecting:

- Balaban lattice block-spin/background-field effective densities to MRS regularized axial-gauge fields;
- Balaban gauge-invariant continuum-observable candidate limits to MRS Schwinger functions;
- their regulator trajectories;
- their gauge fixing;
- their finite-volume/IR-cutoff parameters;
- their limiting probability or distributional objects.

Hence neither ordered composition T_B ; T_M nor T_M ; T_B is typed by the protected records.

### Step 4 — identify route-specific residuals

Since T_B closes large-field/UV stability only on the Balaban route and T_M supplies a different qualified fixed-IR UV interface, the union of the records is not a proof that all common UV obligations of one single construction have been closed.

Therefore D003 must remain route-separated until either one route independently closes its residual obligations or an explicit cross-route equivalence/comparison theorem is proved.

This proves A–D.

## FP1 — invalid route splice

Suppose theorem A proves a uniform estimate for objects X_n in construction X, while theorem B constructs a limit for objects Y_n in construction Y.

Without a theorem identifying X_n with Y_n or providing a comparison that transports A's estimate to Y, the pair A + B does not imply that B's construction satisfies A's estimate.

This exact type failure is the prohibited Balaban–MRS splice.

## FP2 — ultraviolet stability is not continuum uniqueness

A uniformly bounded or precompact family can possess multiple subsequential limits unless an additional uniqueness, Cauchy, or identification theorem is supplied.

Therefore ultraviolet stability may be input to continuum construction without, by itself, proving uniqueness of a continuum theory.

This fixture is abstract; it does not assert the precise compactness topology of Balaban's construction.

## FP3 — fixed-IR UV removal is not infinite volume

A theory may possess a well-defined ultraviolet limit for every fixed infrared cutoff L while the family of those limits has no limit as L tends to infinity.

Therefore UV-cutoff removal at fixed IR does not discharge the D003 infinite-volume obligation.

## Claim boundary

This theorem proves only the composition/dependency statement over protected interfaces.

It does not independently verify the internal proofs of Balaban or MRS, strengthen MRS proof completeness, construct a continuum Yang-Mills theory, remove the IR cutoff, prove OS axioms, set a physical scale, prove a mass gap, or certify any claim.
