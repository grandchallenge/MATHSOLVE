## 1. Source-defined construction datum

Use the construction of Markman, *Secant sheaves on abelian n-folds with real multiplication and Weil classes on abelian 2n-folds with complex multiplication*, arXiv:2509.23079v1.

Fix data in the case of Example 11.2.7 and Lemma 11.2.8:

- `X` is the Jacobian of a genus-four curve with a principal polarization `Theta`;
- `F` is a real quadratic field acting on `X` through rational endomorphisms;
- `q > 0` is chosen so that `K = F(sqrt(-q))` is a CM field of degree four in the biquadratic case treated there;
- the auxiliary `f` and automorphism `g` satisfy the hypotheses imposed in Example 11.2.7;
- `A_0 = X x X-hat` carries the induced embedding `eta : K -> End_Q(A_0)` and a compatible polarization.

Markman constructs two coherent `B`-secant sheaves and an Orlov/Fourier-Mukai object

```text
E_0 = Phi(F_1 boxtimes F_2^vee) in D^b(A_0)
```

with nonzero rank. Its normalized characteristic class

```text
kappa(E_0) = ch(E_0) exp(-c_1(E_0)/rank(E_0))
```

has a degree-four component with nonzero projection to the Weil subspace `HW(A_0,eta)`, and its flat deformation remains of Hodge type under the Weil-type deformations considered in the source.

The source explicitly does **not** establish the semiregularity of `E_0`.

## 2. Exact restricted variety class

Let `Omega` be the connected period-domain component of polarized Weil-type deformations of the source datum `(A_0,eta,h)` used by Markman.

Define `C_CM4` to be the Hodge-generic members `(A,eta,h)` of this component for which the degree-four rational Hodge classes are exactly

```text
Hdg^4(A,Q)
  = Im[Sym^2(Hdg^2(A,Q)) -> H^4(A,Q)]
```

Equivalently, the target is restricted to points whose degree-four Hodge classes are generated by divisor products and the Weil subspace, with no additional special-fiber Hodge classes.

This is a deliberate quantifier restriction. No statement is made for the special Hodge loci where the Hodge group drops and extra rational Hodge classes appear.

## 4. Reduction of an arbitrary input class

Take `(A,eta,h)` in `C_CM4` and arbitrary

```text
alpha in Hdg^4(A,Q).
```

By definition of the restricted class, write

```text
alpha = delta + gamma,
```

with

```text
delta in Im Sym^2(Hdg^2(A,Q)),
gamma in HW(A,eta).
```

### 4.1 Divisor-product summand

By Lefschetz `(1,1)`, every rational class in `Hdg^2(A,Q)` is the rational class of a divisor. Products of two divisor classes are codimension-two algebraic cycle classes. Therefore every `delta` has a preimage in `CH^2(A) tensor Q`.

This does not extrapolate Lefschetz `(1,1)` to the Weil summand.

### 4.2 Weil summand

For a Weil-type pair in the source construction,

```text
HW(A,eta)
```

is one-dimensional as a `K`-vector space. The `K` action is induced by rational endomorphisms of the abelian variety; their graph correspondences are algebraic. Therefore one nonzero algebraic class in `HW(A,eta)` is sufficient to generate every rational Weil class.

Consequently `HC-R021-A8-CM4-C2` reduces to the following construction problem:

> transport one nonzero algebraic Weil component of `kappa_2(E_0)` from the algebraic base object through every point of the selected Hodge-generic component by an explicit deformation theorem.

Once that is done, subtract the algebraic divisor-product component of `kappa_2` to obtain a nonzero algebraic Weil class, generate the full `HW` by algebraic `K` correspondences, and add the divisor-product preimage of `delta`.

## 6. Active obstruction: algebraicity transport

The source states that algebraicity of the transported Weil classes would follow if the Fourier-Mukai object were semiregular in the required sense, and also states that this semiregularity has not been established.

This is the live `HC-O013` deformation firewall.

The missing implication is not

```text
Hodge type -> algebraic.
```

It is the much narrower deformation statement

```text
algebraic object at the base
+ persistent Hodge normalized Chern class
+ adequate semiregularity/unobstructedness
-> deforming algebraic object
-> algebraic normalized Chern class on the target fiber.
```

Without the semiregularity/unobstructedness bridge, the route stops.

## 7. Solve lemma: first-order weak-semiregularity reduction

### HC-R021-L001

Let `Y` be a smooth projective variety and let `E` be a perfect complex for which the ambient deformation evaluation/Atiyah map and Buchweitz-Flenner semiregularity map are defined:

```text
ev_E : HH^2(Y) -> Ext^2(E,E),
sigma_E : Ext^2(E,E) -> SR(E).
```

Assume that

```text
sigma_E | Im(ev_E)
```

is injective. Let `xi` be a first-order ambient deformation direction for which the relevant Chern-character variation vanishes in the semiregularity target. Then the ambient obstruction vanishes:

```text
ev_E(xi) = 0.
```

### Proof

Buchweitz-Flenner semiregularity identifies the semiregularity image of the ambient obstruction with the first-order failure of the Chern character to remain of Hodge type. Hence the Hodge-preserving hypothesis gives

```text
sigma_E(ev_E(xi)) = 0.
```

But `ev_E(xi)` belongs to `Im(ev_E)`, and `sigma_E` is injective on this image. Therefore

```text
ev_E(xi) = 0.
```

QED.

### Scope

This is a first-order reduction only. It does not prove semiregularity of Markman's `E_0`, it does not prove an all-orders deformation, and it does not produce a relative algebraic cycle.

Pridham's 2024 semiregularity theorem strengthens the obstruction-theoretic background: semiregularity maps annihilate actual obstructions, and under ambient deformation they measure failure of the Chern character to remain Hodge. For this campaign, that result sharpens the next question but does not by itself close Markman's weaker-injectivity problem. The missing point remains an all-orders identification/control of the actual obstruction space sufficient to turn weak injectivity on the relevant image into a deformation of the specific object.
