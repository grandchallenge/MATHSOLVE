## Proof-dependency reconstruction from original author presentation

For the 2025 nonextension paper, the primary presentation supplies a useful provisional proof spine (but is not the refereed proof): parameterize possible third entries c of the Type B pair {3,6}; impose the fourth-entry constraints via simultaneous Pell equations; resolve the small c cases (25,875,29750); show recurrence intersections require indices m<n<1.31m and m>sqrt(C); use a Baker–Wüstholz bound to force m<1.6348e20, hence finite third-entry index k<=29; use GP/Pari Baker–Davenport reduction to m<=6, contradicting the lower bound above 201, and exclude zero-index cases. The slides distinguish a Sage integral-point subcomputation from the GP/Pari reduction; neither is independently replayed here. Slide page/lemma labels are not automatically the publication's labels.
