# Next analytic target

## `BSD-R2-A1-2RECIPROCITY-LENGTH`

The quadratic descent ambiguity is now closed. The next target is to connect the exact 2-primary arithmetic length to the analytic leading term.

### Auxiliary-quadratic lane

For an imaginary quadratic `K/Q` satisfying explicitly stated Heegner hypotheses and with every prime dividing `2N` split, and with twist `E^d` of analytic rank zero, establish an integral rank-one reciprocity/length identity that supplies:

1. the exact ordinary 2-primary Selmer or Iwasawa characteristic length over `K`;
2. an explicit reciprocity or p-adic Gross-Zagier comparison valid at `2`;
3. the exact relation between the relevant 2-adic height and the WP00 Neron-Tate regulator normalization;
4. all Euler, period, Tamagawa, isogeny, and interpolation corrections with their `ord_2` values;
5. a source-backed rank-zero 2-primary input for the twist, or an exact retained twist defect if that input is unavailable.

Then combine that result with the WP06 Sha-length identity to solve for the base-curve 2-primary Sha length and compare it with `ord_2(L'(E,1)/(Omega_E Reg_E))`.

### Direct-Q alternative

A direct ordinary 2-adic theorem over `Q` that determines the same finite length and reciprocity normalization may bypass the quadratic lane.

### Stop rule

Do not call `BSD-R2-A1` proved until the analytic-to-arithmetic length equality and every WP00 normalization correction have both been discharged. Descent control alone is not the leading-term theorem.
