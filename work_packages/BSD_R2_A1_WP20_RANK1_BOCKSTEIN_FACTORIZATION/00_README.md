# BSD-R2-A1-WP20 — rank-one determinant/Bockstein factorization

## Purpose

WP19 reduced the selected target to the intrinsic rank-one first-Fitting equality

`delta_2(E) = v_2(Fitt^1_{Z_2}(X_E))`,

where

`X_E := Sel_{2^infinity}^{Kum}(E/Q)^vee`.

WP20 makes one further representation change. It proves a pure algebra theorem showing that whenever a one-parameter determinant specializes to a rank-one module, its first derivative factors exactly into:

1. the intrinsic first Fitting ideal of the specialized module; and
2. a one-dimensional Bockstein scalar measuring motion of the free kernel into the free cokernel.

For `R=Z_2`, this gives the exact valuation identity

`v_2(first determinant derivative)
 = v_2(Fitt^1_R(M)) + v_2(Bockstein scalar)`.

This theorem is independent of BSD, Kato, Mazur-Tate, Iwasawa theory, or any external source.

- **determinant realization:** construct an exact literal-`p=2` determinant/Selmer presentation whose rank-one specialization is the protected primitive Kummer module `X_E` or an exactly comparable object;

If both are supplied, WP20 mechanically recovers the WP19 first-Fitting equality.

## Material theorem

Let `R` be a DVR with fraction field `K`, let `S=R[[T]]`, and let

`A(T) in M_m(S)`

with `rank_K A(0)=m-1`.

Set

`M := coker(A(0):R^m->R^m)`.

Then `M` has rank one. Let

`beta_A : ker A(0) -> M/Tor_R(M)`

be induced by the coefficient `A_1` of `T` in `A(T)`.

Both source and target are free rank one. Let `B_A subset R` be the principal ideal generated by the scalar of `beta_A` in any primitive bases.

WP20 proves

`(coeff_T det A(T))
 = Fitt^1_R(M) * B_A`

as ideals of `R`.

For `R=Z_2`, whenever the coefficient is nonzero,

`ord_2(coeff_T det A(T))
 = v_2(Fitt^1_{Z_2}(M)) + v_2(B_A)`.

The theorem includes the torsion-free case and is invariant under invertible power-series changes of presentation.
