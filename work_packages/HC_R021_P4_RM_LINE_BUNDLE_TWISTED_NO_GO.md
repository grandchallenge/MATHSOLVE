## 1. Purpose

Close the apparent escape left after `L016`, `L025-L028`, and the non-ample companion decomposition `L035`.

`L016` rules out **split** line-bundle complexes on the `beta'` ray. `L025-L028` rule out several natural non-split divisor realizations. `L035` identifies the rational non-ample RM companion class and suggests using index-two line bundles, whose `Ext^2` groups are nonzero, as genuinely derived bridges.

The latter idea fails for a structural degree reason.

For two distinct RM line-bundle classes on an abelian fourfold, the nonzero Ext groups in the two directions occur in complementary indices summing to `4`. A degree-one differential entry and a degree-one opposite homotopy entry in a twisted complex would require underlying Ext degrees summing to `2`. Hence no first-order nullhomotopy can transfer a diagonal obstruction between distinct RM line-bundle sectors.

Within one isomorphism class of line bundle, a sector of nonzero Euler multiplicity retains every nonzero line-bundle characteristic obstruction by the degree-zero trace component of semiregularity.

Therefore a finite twisted complex made solely from shifted RM line bundles cannot annihilate the eight target directions unless every active line-bundle class annihilates them termwise. `L016` proves that no such line bundle exists.

## 2. RM line-bundle differences are nondegenerate

Let `NS_RM(X)_Q` denote the two-dimensional rational Neron-Severi subspace generated by the real-multiplication polarizations. A nonzero class in this plane is represented over the two real embeddings by

```text
r_1 U + r_2 V,
```

with `r_1,r_2` the two conjugates of a nonzero element of the real quadratic field `F`.

Neither conjugate can vanish. Hence the associated alternating form on `H^1(X)` is nondegenerate and its fourth power is nonzero.

Consequently, if `L_i,L_j` are line bundles whose Chern classes lie in the RM plane and

```text
c1(L_i)-c1(L_j) != 0,
```

then

```text
M_ij:=L_i tensor L_j^(-1)
```

is a nondegenerate line bundle. Tensoring by a degree-zero line bundle does not change its index.

## 3. Index theorem on the abelian fourfold

For a nondegenerate line bundle `M` on an abelian variety there is a unique index

```text
i(M)
```

such that

```text
H^p(X,M)=0 for p!=i(M).
```

On the fourfold,

```text
i(M^(-1))=4-i(M).                         (3.1)
```

For an RM-plane class the two real blocks have multiplicity two, so the possible indices are

```text
0,2,4.
```

Therefore, for two distinct RM line-bundle classes,

```text
Ext^p(L_j,L_i) !=0
```

forces

```text
p=i(M_ij),
```

while

```text
Ext^q(L_i,L_j) !=0
```

forces

```text
q=4-p.                                    (3.2)
```

Thus any nonzero opposite pair of Ext groups between distinct RM slopes has underlying degrees summing to `4`.

If the two line bundles have the same first Chern class but differ by a nontrivial element of `Pic^0(X)`, all cohomology of their quotient vanishes and there are no Ext groups in either direction.

The only remaining case is that the line bundles are actually isomorphic up to the chosen labeling.

## 4. Degree-one twisted-complex entries require sum two

Let a finite twisted complex have constituents

```text
L_i[n_i].
```

A total degree-one entry from `L_j[n_j]` to `L_i[n_i]` is represented by an underlying class

```text
d_ij in Ext^p(L_j,L_i),
p=1+n_i-n_j.                               (4.1)
```

An opposite total degree-one homotopy entry is represented by

```text
h_ji in Ext^q(L_i,L_j),
q=1+n_j-n_i.                               (4.2)
```

Hence necessarily

```text
p+q=2.                                    (4.3)
```

Compare (4.3) with (3.2). If the two Chern classes are distinct, nonzero opposite Ext groups require `p+q=4`; total degree-one differential/homotopy entries require `p+q=2`.

Therefore

```text
for c1(L_i)!=c1(L_j):
  d_ij !=0  =>  every opposite degree-one h_ji=0,
```

and conversely. In particular

```text
d_ij h_ji=h_ji d_ij=0                     (4.4)
```

for every pair of distinct RM line-bundle sectors, independently of the length of the twisted complex.

This is exactly the pairwise expression appearing in the diagonal of a commutator `[d,h]`.

## 5. Diagonal obstruction cannot move between RM slopes

Let

```text
E=(direct_sum_i L_i[n_i],d)
```

be any finite twisted complex of RM line bundles. Let `xi in HT^2(X)` and write the characteristic action on a constituent as

```text
o_i(xi)=ev_(L_i)(xi) in Ext^2(L_i,L_i)=H^2(O_X).
```

If `ob_E(xi)=0`, there must be a total degree-one endomorphism `h` such that, in the chosen enhancement,

```text
[d,h]=o(xi)                                (5.1)
```

up to the standard representative corrections. The diagonal `i,i` entry of the commutator is a sum

```text
sum_j d_ij h_ji +- h_ij d_ji.             (5.2)
```

By Section 4, every term in (5.2) involving a distinct RM first Chern class is zero. Therefore cancellation of the diagonal class on an `L_i` constituent can use only constituents isomorphic to `L_i` itself.

Thus the first-order nullhomotopy problem splits into isomorphism-class sectors even when the twisted differential itself connects different slopes in one direction.

## 6. A nonzero Euler sector retains a nonzero line-bundle obstruction

Fix one isomorphism class `L` and let `E_L` be the full subcollection of copies of shifts of `L`. Its virtual multiplicity is

```text
m_L:=sum_(copies j of L) (-1)^(n_j).
```

The Chern character contribution is

```text
ch(E_L)=m_L exp(c1(L)).                    (6.1)
```

For a line bundle the degree-zero component of the Buchweitz-Flenner semiregularity map on

```text
Ext^2(L,L)=H^2(O_X)
```

is the ordinary trace, hence the identity on `H^2(O_X)` up to the multiplicity of the vector-space factor.

If the common constituent obstruction

```text
o_L(xi)=ev_L(xi)
```

were nullhomotopic on `E_L`, its semiregularity trace would vanish. The filtration-zero trace is

```text
m_L o_L(xi).                               (6.2)
```

Over characteristic zero, if

```text
m_L !=0,
```

then (6.2) vanishes only when

```text
o_L(xi)=0.                                (6.3)
```

Sectors with `m_L=0` contribute zero to the total Chern character. By Section 5 they cannot transfer their nullhomotopies to a different RM slope, so they cannot repair an active sector.

### HC-R021-L036a — sectorwise obstruction criterion

For a finite twisted complex of shifted RM line bundles and any ambient degree-two class `xi`, if `ob_E(xi)=0`, then

```text
ev_L(xi)=0
```

for every line-bundle isomorphism class `L` occurring with nonzero virtual multiplicity.

## 7. Apply the eight target directions

Assume now that

```text
ch(E)=M beta',
M!=0,
```

and that all eight target classes satisfy

```text
ob_E(k_a)=0,
1<=a<=8.                                  (7.1)
```

At least one line-bundle sector has nonzero virtual multiplicity. By `L036a`, every active sector must satisfy

```text
ev_L(k_a)=0,
1<=a<=8.                                  (7.2)
```

But `HC-R021-L016` proves that no nonzero line bundle can annihilate all eight target directions. In fact vanishing of `k_1,...,k_6` forces

```text
c1(L)=A U+B V
```

and then

```text
ev_L(k_7)=(qa+A^2) alpha_1 !=0,
ev_L(k_8)=(qb+B^2) alpha_2 !=0.
```

This contradicts (7.2).

Therefore no such twisted complex exists.

## 8. HC-R021-L036 — all finite RM-line-bundle twisted complexes are pruned

### Statement

Let `E` be any finite twisted complex whose graded constituents are shifted line bundles with first Chern classes in the rational RM Neron-Severi plane, allowing arbitrary degree-zero twists and arbitrary Maurer-Cartan differential entries permitted by the derived category.

If

```text
ch(E)=M beta', M!=0,
```

then `E` cannot satisfy

```text
ob_E(k_a)=0 for all a=1,...,8.
```

Equivalently, no such object has second-factor obstruction rank `20`.

This includes:

- split line-bundle realizations (`L016`);
- arbitrary two-term line-bundle totalizations (`L025`);
- longer complexes with `K_0`-zero line-bundle bridges;
- complexes using the non-ample index-two RM companion divisor of `L035`;
- arbitrary shifts and nonzero `Ext^2` twisting classes between distinct index-two RM line bundles.

### Proof

Sections 2-7.

QED.

## 9. Structural consequence

The non-ample companion class of `L035` remains a useful cohomological coordinate, but its categorification cannot be achieved successfully by line bundles alone.

The surviving rank-20 object must contain at least one genuinely non-line-bundle constituent whose opposite Ext profile is not constrained by the line-bundle index relation

```text
p+q=4.
```

High-value candidates are therefore narrowed to:

1. higher-rank non-IT vector bundles/full-support complexes;
2. torsion-free divisorial modules with nontrivial singularity Ext algebra;
3. surface/curve constituents participating in a genuinely higher global composition;
4. a derived object obtained from a non-line-bundle secant construction such as Markman's partial-normalization complex of Example 8.2.3.

## 10. Scope and firewall

`L036` does **not** rule out:

- higher-rank vector bundles;
- coherent sheaves not filtered by line bundles;
- non-line-bundle semihomogeneous or non-IT bundles;
- torsion/intersection-supported constituents;
- Markman's partial-normalization secant complex;
- higher products involving such constituents;
- second-factor rank `20`;
- all-orders algebraicity transport;
- `HC-R021`;
- the Hodge conjecture.
