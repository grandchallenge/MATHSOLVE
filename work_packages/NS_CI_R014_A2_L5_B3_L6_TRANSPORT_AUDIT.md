## 1. Fixed-cutoff tail

For fixed `q`, set

```math
V_q=P_{>q}u,
\qquad
L_q=P_{\le q-2}u,
\qquad
\Phi(V)=|V|^4V.
```

After eliminating pressure with the Leray projector `mathbb P`, the fixed-tail
equation contains

```math
P_{>q}\mathbb P(L_q\cdot\nabla V_q)
```

as its principal low--high transport contribution.

If the projection were absent, incompressibility would give the exact identity

```math
\langle L_q\cdot\nabla V_q,\Phi(V_q)\rangle
=
\frac16\int L_q\cdot\nabla |V_q|^6dx
=0.
```

This is the motivation for testing `L6` energy rather than another differentiated
H1 energy estimate.

## 2. The actual projected term

The term in the projected equation is instead

```math
I_q
=
\langle
P_{>q}\mathbb P(L_q\cdot\nabla V_q),
\Phi(V_q)
\rangle.
```

Adding and subtracting the unprojected transport and using the preceding exact
cancellation gives

```math
I_q
=
\langle
(P_{>q}\mathbb P-I)(L_q\cdot\nabla V_q),
\Phi(V_q)
\rangle.
```

Thus the hoped-for transport cancellation does not eliminate the principal term;
it converts it exactly into a high-pass/Leray residual. Two structural facts
prevent the residual from vanishing:

1. `Phi(V_q)` is neither divergence-free nor confined to frequencies above
   `lambda_q`; nonlinear multiplication creates low frequencies.
2. `mathbb P` does not commute with multiplication by `L_q`; the low--high
   pressure component is generated by derivatives of the low field.

## 3. Shellwise commutator scale

For a smooth dyadic projector `P_p` with `p>q+O(1)`, the transport commutator has
kernel representation

```math
[P_p,L_q\cdot\nabla]V_p(x)
=
\int K_p(x-y)
\bigl(L_q(x)-L_q(y)\bigr)
\cdot\nabla V_p(y)dy.
```

The mean-value theorem supplies one factor `lambda_p^{-1} ||grad L_q||_infinity`,
while frequency localization of `V_p` supplies `||grad V_p||_r` at scale
`lambda_p ||V_p||_r`. The scale factors cancel, leaving

```math
\|[P_p,L_q\cdot\nabla]V_p\|_r
\lesssim
G_q\|V_p\|_r,
```

where

```math
G_q(t)=\|\nabla L_q(t)\|_\infty.
```

The Leray/pressure part has the same low-deformation structure after paraproduct
localization; Riesz transforms are order zero and the low--high pressure source
contains a derivative of the low field. Consequently the direct `L6` energy
ledger has schematic principal remainder

```math
|I_q|
\lesssim
G_q\|V_q\|_6^6
+
\text{finite-neighbour / strictly-high terms}.
```

The exact constants are immaterial to the obstruction: the candidate was meant
to remove the low deformation coefficient, and it does not.

## 4. Why the selected hypothesis does not control the coefficient

For `q=Q(t)`, the defining strict-high bound applies only to shells `p>Q`.
It gives no upper bound on

```math
G_Q
=\|\nabla u_{\le Q-2}\|_\infty.
```

Replacing `G_Q` by an imported integrable low-mode coefficient would reproduce
the already excluded `f in L1_t` route. Localizing the fixed-`q` estimate to
`{Q=q}` also recreates selector variation; keeping `q` fixed globally loses
strict-high absorption on `{Q>q}`. These are the same protected interfaces
identified in L5-4, reached here from a genuinely different `L6` transport
calculation.
