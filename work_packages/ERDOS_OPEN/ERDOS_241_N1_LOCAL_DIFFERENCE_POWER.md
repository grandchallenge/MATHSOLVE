# ERDOS-241-N1 — quantitative power of the local difference obstructions

## Protected starting point

For a strong-B3 set \(A\subseteq\{1,\dots,N\}\), the protected R1+A1 synthesis has independently replayed the exact finite values through \(N=64\) and admitted the following internal structural facts:

1. strong B3 implies the ordinary B2/Sidon property, hence every positive difference \(a-b\) with \(a>b\) is uniquely represented by its ordered pair;
2. if \(\Delta(A)=\{a-b:a>b,\ a,b\in A\}\), then \(\Delta(A)\cap 2\Delta(A)=\varnothing\);
3. if \(d_1=a-b\) and \(d_2=c-d\) come from vertex-disjoint pairs, then \(d_1+d_2\notin\Delta(A)\);
4. naive superadditive, multiplicative, monotone-normalized-ratio, greedy-extension, and adjacent-block-union routes are invalid.

The protected packet states a leading upper constant \((7/2)^{1/3}\), but S1 has not yet returned. Therefore that literature-dependent constant is a protected starting datum only; this work package must not assert currency or novelty.

## Exact assignment

Determine the quantitative power of the confirmed local difference constraints before importing a larger Fourier or source-dependent apparatus.

Prove one of the following two bounded outcomes.

### Route A — useful inequality

Using only standard mathematics and the admitted internal facts above, derive an explicit cubic-scale inequality
\[
  |A|^3 \le cN + o(N)
\]
with a completely explicit \(c<7/2\), or another theorem-grade inequality that strictly improves the protected constant-gap route after normalization.

### Route B — local-constraint no-go

Construct an infinite family of finite integer sets or an exact realizable difference-system model satisfying the admitted local constraints (unique positive differences, no doubled difference, and no sum of vertex-disjoint differences returning to the difference set) while having cardinality too large for those constraints alone to imply any \(c<7/2\) cubic bound. The construction must make precise which strong-B3 axiom it drops. This establishes that the local constraints are insufficient and identifies the first missing global statistic.

## Success criterion

Return exactly one theorem-grade result:

- LOCAL_CONSTRAINT_BOUND_IMPROVED, with the full inequality and proof; or
- LOCAL_CONSTRAINTS_INSUFFICIENT, with an explicit infinite countermodel/family and proof; or
- EXACT_SMALLER_MISSING_LEMMA, only if the reduction is strict and the missing lemma is stated quantitatively.

A computation may guide the proof but cannot be the sole basis for an asymptotic claim.
