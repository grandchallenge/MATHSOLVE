## 4. Why the new target is not a restatement of the raw LP

## 5. Attack decomposition

### E3-L01 — lifted finite obstruction / valid-cut extraction

Start from the exact ((4,7)) obstruction. Identify the smallest family of configuration-level incompatibilities sufficient to exclude every ((5,4,4,4)) profile. The desired output is not another exhaustive enumeration but a human-checkable finite certificate or a reusable lifted valid inequality.

Primary question:
[
	ext{what combinatorial feature distinguishes the impossible 17-point profile from the feasible balanced 16-point profile?}
]

The strongest useful result would identify a finite motif whose occurrence is forced in any four near-extremal fibres and whose carry placements are incompatible.

### E3-Q02 — quadratic forcing interface

Make the S04 interface quantitative and fibre-labelled. Given four dense locally 4-AP-free fibres satisfying the carry-compatibility constraints, prove a precise dichotomy:
- either a cross-fibre 4-AP exists; or
- at least one fibre has degree-2 / (U^3) structure above an explicit polynomial threshold.

The task must keep track of the exact four-function system generated by the D01 carry templates.

A mere one-set (U^3) inverse theorem citation is insufficient.

### E3-T01 — structure-to-transversal incompatibility

Assume the structured side of E3-Q02. Determine whether the quadratic structures forced on several labelled fibres can coexist across all 21 carry templates.

The target is a deletion/transversal statement of the form

]
for some (	heta<1), where (eta) is the fibre density scale and (	au_{	ext{carry}}) is the minimum number of point deletions needed to destroy all forbidden cross-fibre templates.

This is the asymptotic heart of the tranche.

### E3-A04 — adversarial falsification

Search for an explicit family of four locally near-extremal 4-AP-free fibres with

- recursive constructions suggested by the lower-transfer theorem from E3-A03.

A genuine counterexample to the (L=2) formulation is a successful tranche outcome; it would redirect the campaign to larger fixed (L) or to the weaker persistence route.

## 6. Native synthesis rule

The four work packages are evidence lanes, not voting lanes.

Native synthesis shall select the smallest statement supported by the combined returns. It may:
- prove or reduce E3-B-FOUR-FIBRE-DEFICIT;
- formulate a strictly smaller named bridge;
- falsify the (L=2) route;
- or declare an exact source/structure blocker.

It may not promote `E3-Q4-GLUING-RADIUS` until the separate `E3-V03` verification gate is satisfied.

## 7. Immediate success criteria

A useful tranche does **not** require a proof of Erdős Problem 3.

Success is any one of:

1. a theorem-grade four-fibre deficit bound with exponent (1+	heta<2);
2. a theorem-grade finite-to-asymptotic bridge reducing that bound to a named quadratic/configuration statement;
3. a rigorous counterexample to the (L=2) route;
4. a sharply smaller obstruction whose resolution is materially narrower than the current generic “quadratic stability/transversal theorem” gap.

## 8. Non-goals

This tranche must not:
- infer asymptotic truth from the (N=7) finite obstruction;

- replace four labelled fibres by a one-set recurrence theorem without proving the transfer;
- treat generic (eta^4N) deletion estimates as sufficient for an exponent below (2);
- treat a silent verifier lease as a mathematical blocker;
- promote unverified G01 claims into protected theorem status.
