# E3-X01 — renormalization inequality search for r_4

Frontier node: `E3-Q4-SERIES`.

Seek a theorem-grade cross-scale inequality for

[
a_n:=r_4(2^n)/2^n
]

that is stronger than monotonicity and, together with known decay
(a_nll n^{-c}), would imply (sum_n a_n<infty).

Candidate families include, but are not limited to:

- quasi-submultiplicative inequalities;
- density-loss inequalities across nested scales;
- averaged contraction over a bounded number of scale steps;
- inequalities obtained from local (U^3), energy-decrement, or density-increment
  structure.

Every proposed inequality must be proved or explicitly falsified. Do not infer
an inequality merely from the existence of a density-increment iteration.

Completion: PROVED_ADVANCE, COUNTEREXAMPLE, or SMALLEST_MISSING_LEMMA.
