# E3-A02 — adversarial attack on cross-scale upgrades

Frontier node: `E3-Q4-SERIES`.

Attack natural attempts to upgrade the known pointwise bound into series
convergence.

In particular:

1. test naive submultiplicativity and quasi-submultiplicativity claims for
   (r_4) or (a_n);
2. test whether partitioning a dyadic interval into shorter intervals can
   improve the logarithmic exponent rather than merely reproduce it;
3. use known product/lower-bound constructions where applicable to rule out
   overstrong recurrences;
4. identify exactly which proposed cross-scale inequalities remain viable.

Completion: COUNTEREXAMPLE, BOUNDARY_SHARPENED, or NO_DEFECT_IN_DECLARED_REGION.
