## Attack directions

Investigate, in order:

1. **Common quadratic factor incompatibility.**
   If multiple fibres correlate with quadratic phases/factors, substitute those phases into the carry equations. Determine whether the 21 templates force inconsistent phase relations unless one fibre loses substantial mass.

2. **Energy increment / rank accumulation.**
   If every compatible labelled quartet forces a structured fibre, iterate across many carry templates. Can bounded ambient quadratic complexity absorb all constraints, or must rank/energy grow beyond what a near-extremal fibre can support?

3. **Configuration transversals.**

4. **Finite motif amplification.**
   Test whether the exact (N=7) C01 obstruction is the smallest instance of a motif that appears with positive density inside structured fibres and amplifies to a polynomial deletion cost.
