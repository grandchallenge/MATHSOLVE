## Strongest exact statement

Let
\[
G=\mathbb Z/P\mathbb Z
\]
with normalized Fourier transform
\[
\widehat f(\xi)
=
\mathbb E_{x\in G}f(x)e_P(-\xi x),
\qquad
e_P(t):=e^{2\pi i t/P}.
\]

Let
\[
b:G\to\mathbb C
\]
be any function, and for a shift
\[
e\in G
\]
define the multiplicative derivative
\[
\Delta_e b(x):=b(x)b(x+e).
\]

For every derivative shift \(e\) and spatial Fourier frequency \(\xi\), put
\[
A_e(\xi)
:=
\widehat{\Delta_e b}(\xi).
\]

Then the dependence of \(A_e(\xi)\) on the **shift parameter** \(e\) is exactly
\[
\boxed{
A_e(\xi)
=
\sum_{\eta\in G}
\widehat b(\xi-\eta)\widehat b(\eta)e_P(\eta e).
}
\]

Equivalently, taking Fourier transform in the shift variable \(e\),
\[
\boxed{
\widehat{A_\bullet(\xi)}(\eta)
=
\widehat b(\xi-\eta)\widehat b(\eta).
}
\]

Thus the derivative-spectrum trajectory
\[
e\longmapsto \widehat{\Delta_e b}(\xi)
\]
is a trigonometric polynomial whose shift-frequencies are exactly the additive pairs
\[
(\eta,\xi-\eta)
\]
from the base Fourier spectrum of \(b\).

Define the nonnegative pair-energy measure
\[
\mu_\xi(\eta)
:=
|\widehat b(\xi-\eta)|^2|\widehat b(\eta)|^2.
\]

Then:

### Shift-variable Parseval

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_e|A_e(\xi)|^2
=
\sum_{\eta}\mu_\xi(\eta).
}
\]

### Exact shift autocorrelation

For every
\[
h\in G,
\]
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E_e
A_e(\xi)\overline{A_{e+h}(\xi)}
=
\sum_{\eta}
\mu_\xi(\eta)e_P(-\eta h).
}
\]

Hence the shift-autocorrelation of one derivative Fourier mode is exactly the Fourier transform of a **nonnegative** measure on the base-frequency pair parameter \(\eta\).

### Total derivative spectral energy

Summing over the spatial frequency,
\[
\boxed{
\sum_{\xi}
\mathbb E_e|A_e(\xi)|^2
=
\left(\sum_{\eta}|\widehat b(\eta)|^2\right)^2
=
\|b\|_2^4.
}
\]

Therefore all derivative-frequency transport across all shifts and all \(\xi\) is generated by a fixed finite energy budget coming from the base spectrum of the same physical fibre.

## Exact concentration-versus-diffuseness interface

For a fixed \(\xi\), write
\[
M_\xi
:=
\sum_\eta\mu_\xi(\eta)
=
\mathbb E_e|A_e(\xi)|^2.
\]

Fix
\[
0<\tau\le1.
\]

Then exactly one of the following alternatives holds.

### A. Pair-frequency concentration

There exists
\[
\eta_\ast\in G
\]
such that
\[
\boxed{
\mu_\xi(\eta_\ast)
\ge
\tau M_\xi.
}
\]

Equivalently, the projection of the derivative trajectory
\[
e\mapsto A_e(\xi)
\]
onto the single shift-character
\[
e_P(\eta_\ast e)
\]
carries at least a \(\tau\) fraction of its total \(L^2(e)\) energy.

If
\[
c_\ast
:=
\widehat b(\xi-\eta_\ast)\widehat b(\eta_\ast),
\]
then
\[
\mathbb E_e
\left|
A_e(\xi)-c_\ast e_P(\eta_\ast e)
\right|^2
=
M_\xi-\mu_\xi(\eta_\ast)
\le
(1-\tau)M_\xi.
\]

Thus when \(\tau\) is close to \(1\), the derivative Fourier mode has coherent approximately linear phase transport in the derivative shift \(e\).

### B. Pair-frequency diffuseness

For every
\[
\eta\in G,
\]
\[
\boxed{
\mu_\xi(\eta)<\tau M_\xi.
}
\]

Equivalently every shift-frequency coefficient of
\[
e\mapsto A_e(\xi)
\]
has squared magnitude less than a \(\tau\) fraction of its total shift-energy.

No single base-frequency pair controls the derivative-frequency trajectory.

This dichotomy is purely algebraic and has no loss.

## Proof

Expand \(b\) by Fourier inversion:
\[
b(x)
=
\sum_r\widehat b(r)e_P(rx),
\]
and
\[
b(x+e)
=
\sum_s\widehat b(s)e_P(sx)e_P(se).
\]

Therefore
\[
\Delta_e b(x)
=
\sum_{r,s}
\widehat b(r)\widehat b(s)
e_P((r+s)x)e_P(se).
\]

Taking the Fourier coefficient at \(\xi\) enforces
\[
r+s=\xi,
\]
so
\[
A_e(\xi)
=
\sum_s
\widehat b(\xi-s)\widehat b(s)e_P(se).
\]

Rename \(s\) as \(\eta\). This proves
\[
A_e(\xi)
=
\sum_\eta
\widehat b(\xi-\eta)\widehat b(\eta)e_P(\eta e).
\]

The shift-variable Fourier coefficient is therefore exactly
\[
\widehat b(\xi-\eta)\widehat b(\eta).
\]

Parseval in \(e\) gives
\[
\mathbb E_e|A_e(\xi)|^2
=
\sum_\eta
|\widehat b(\xi-\eta)|^2|\widehat b(\eta)|^2
=
\sum_\eta\mu_\xi(\eta).
\]

For the shift autocorrelation,
\[
A_{e+h}(\xi)
=
\sum_\eta
\widehat b(\xi-\eta)\widehat b(\eta)
e_P(\eta e)e_P(\eta h).
\]

Multiplying by the conjugate and averaging in \(e\) forces equality of the two shift-frequency indices. Hence
\[
\mathbb E_e
A_e(\xi)\overline{A_{e+h}(\xi)}
=
\sum_\eta
|\widehat b(\xi-\eta)|^2|\widehat b(\eta)|^2
e_P(-\eta h).
\]

Finally,
\[
\sum_\xi\sum_\eta
|\widehat b(\xi-\eta)|^2|\widehat b(\eta)|^2
=
\left(\sum_r|\widehat b(r)|^2\right)
\left(\sum_\eta|\widehat b(\eta)|^2\right)
=
\|b\|_2^4.
\]

The concentration/diffuseness alternative is simply the exact decomposition of the nonnegative weights
\[
\mu_\xi(\eta)
\]
relative to the threshold
\[
\tau M_\xi.
\]
