# Frontier-governed research execution

OPENMATH established a reliable controlled interface between outside intelligence and governed knowledge, but its automatic replay-successor rule conflated evidence processing with research scheduling.

GCL-ERDOS3 is the reference reset.

A RESULT/1 return is evidence. Its `Next residual` section is not a scheduler command. After adjudication, a protected frontier gate must choose one of: ADVANCE_FRONTIER, INDEPENDENT_VERIFY, FORMALIZE, ADVERSARIAL_ATTACK, SOURCE_BLOCKED, REFUTED, CLOSED_NO_SUCCESSOR, or CAMPAIGN_TERMINAL.

Default verification budget is one genuinely independent replay of a bounded deterministic claim. Additional replay requires a named reason such as discrepancy, material artifact change, certification, or a deliberately adversarial challenge.

Evidence acceptance and theorem acceptance remain separate. In particular, `claim_effect: NONE` normally closes the operational contribution without generating a mathematically equivalent successor.
