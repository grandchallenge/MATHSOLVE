# WP36 companion lemma — formal cosets are coordinate congruence classes

## Purpose

This lemma supplies the exact quotient-coset justification used in Section 5 of `01_FINITE_FORMAL_NORM_MEMBERSHIP_THEOREM.md`.

No shallow formal logarithm is used.

Let `R` be the valuation ring of one of the finite local cyclotomic layers `L_n/Q_2`, let `m` be its maximal ideal, and let

`F(X,Y) in R[[X,Y]]`

be the elliptic formal group law attached to the fixed minimal integral Weierstrass equation. Let

`i_F(Y) in R[[Y]]`

be the formal inverse, and write

`X minus_F Y := F(X,i_F(Y))`.

### Lemma `BSD-A1-WP36-COORDINATE-COSETS-001`

For `x,y in m`,

`x minus_F y in 4R`

if and only if

`x-y in 4R`.

Consequently the cosets of the formal subgroup

`Ehat(4R) subset Ehat(m)`

are represented exactly by ordinary coordinate residue classes in

`m/4R`.

In particular,

`#(Ehat(m)/Ehat(4R)) = #(m/4R)`.

### Proof

Define

`D_F(X,Y):=F(X,i_F(Y))`.

The formal group axioms give

`D_F(Y,Y)=F(Y,i_F(Y))=0`.

The substitution homomorphism

`R[[X,Y]] -> R[[Y]]`,

`X |-> Y`,

has kernel generated by `X-Y`. Therefore there is a unique

`U_F(X,Y) in R[[X,Y]]`

such that

`D_F(X,Y)=(X-Y)U_F(X,Y)`.

The linear term of a one-dimensional commutative formal group is

`F(X,Y)=X+Y+higher terms`,

and its inverse satisfies

`i_F(Y)=-Y+higher terms`.

Hence

`D_F(X,Y)=X-Y+higher terms`,

so

`U_F(0,0)=1`.

For `x,y in m`, every nonconstant term of `U_F(x,y)-1` lies in `m`. Thus

`U_F(x,y) in 1+m subset R^x`.

Therefore

`x minus_F y
 =(x-y)U_F(x,y)`

belongs to `4R` exactly when `x-y` belongs to `4R`, because multiplication by the unit `U_F(x,y)` preserves the ideal `4R`.

Two formal points represented by `x,y in m` lie in the same coset modulo `Ehat(4R)` exactly when their formal difference lies in `Ehat(4R)`, equivalently when its coordinate lies in `4R`. The preceding equivalence shows this is exactly ordinary congruence

`x congruent y mod 4R`.

Thus the quotient cosets are canonically indexed by `m/4R`, and the cardinalities agree. QED.

## Application to WP36

For `L_n/Q_2` totally ramified of degree `e_n=2^n`,

`4O_{L_n}=m_{L_n}^{2e_n}`

and the residue field has order `2`. Hence

`#(Ehat(m_{L_n})/Ehat(4O_{L_n}))
 = #(m_{L_n}/m_{L_n}^{2e_n})
 = 2^{2e_n-1}`.

Therefore the shallow source quotient used by WP36 contains exactly

- `8` classes for `n=1`;
- `128` classes for `n=2`.

This validates the finite enumeration in `BSD-A1-WP36-NORM-TABLE-001` without invoking any logarithm on `Ehat(m_{L_n})` or `Ehat(2Z_2)`.
