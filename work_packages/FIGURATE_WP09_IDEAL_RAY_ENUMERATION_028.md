# FIGURATE WP09-028 — exact HNF norm-ideal enumeration and targeted oriented-ray membership

**Scope.** This is a complete, exact **targeted ray-class decision for the norm-|N| ideal fibre** of the recurring anchor when both finite enumeration stages finish. It does **not** calculate an entire abstract ray class group, prove a parameter-uniform classification, or confer independent MATHCERT certification. Parent: [FIGURATE WP09 #3145](https://github.com/grandchallenge/MATH-PROGRAMME/issues/3145). Dependencies: protected WP09-023, WP09-025, WP09-026, WP09-027.

## Setup and conductor

For any integer $m\ge0$, let

$$
A=8m+1,\quad D=2A,\quad H=8m(m+1)+1,\quad N=-2(2A-1)H<0.
$$

Use the actual order $\mathcal O=\mathbb Z[\sqrt D]$, which may be nonmaximal: if $A=f^2a_0$ with $a_0$ odd squarefree, then $\mathcal O=\mathbb Z[f\sqrt{2a_0}]$ has conductor $f$. Protected WP09-027 proved $\gcd(A,N)=1$. Thus every ideal of norm $|N|$ is prime to the conductor and is proper and invertible. The oriented ray target is the class of $\rho=((A+1)/2)(1+\sqrt D)$ modulo $A\mathcal O$, with real-sign pattern $(+,-)$.

## Theorem A — complete ideal HNF enumeration

Fix positive $n$ and $D>1$. Every integral ideal $I$ of $\mathcal O$ with index $n$ has a unique lattice basis

$$
I=\left\langle (ch,0),(cj,c)\right\rangle_{\mathbb Z}
$$

where $c,h$ are positive integers, $0\le j<h$, and

$$
c^2h=n,\qquad j^2\equiv D\pmod h.
$$

Conversely every such triple defines an ideal of index $n$. If $\gcd(n,f)=1$, these are exactly the invertible norm-$n$ ideals relevant here.

**Proof.** An integral lattice of finite index in the basis $1,\sqrt D$ has unique HNF generators $(a,0),(b,c)$ with $a,c>0$, $0\le b<a$, determinant $ac=n$. Stability under multiplication by $\sqrt D$ on $(a,0)$ forces $c\mid a$ and $c\mid b$. Writing $a=ch,b=cj$, stability of the second generator $(cj,c)$ is equivalent to $h\mid(D-j^2)$. Since $\mathcal O$ is generated by $\sqrt D$, these two conditions are also sufficient for ideal stability. The determinant is $c^2h$. Uniqueness follows from unique lattice HNF. QED.

The exact membership test for an element $\alpha=x+y\sqrt D$ is

$$
\alpha\in I\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
c\mid y\quad\text{and}\quad ch\mid(x-jy).
$$

If $|N_{K/\mathbb Q}(\alpha)|=n$, then $(\alpha)$ has index $n$. Consequently membership $\alpha\in I$ implies $(\alpha)=I$, since both ideals have index $n$.

## Theorem B — complete classification relative to one oriented ray class

Enumerate all norm-$|N|$ ideals by Theorem A. Separately enumerate **all** positive norm-$N$ Pell representatives in the complete fundamental-unit region proved in WP09-023, applied to the normalized equation of WP09-025. Each representative $\alpha=x+y\sqrt D$ generates exactly one enumerated ideal by Theorem A. Every generator of norm $N$ with sign pattern $(+,-)$ is in the positive norm-one-unit orbit of one of those representatives, after sufficiently many unit steps to make $x,y>0$.

For each representative, apply the protected WP09-026 **two-gcd necessary-and-sufficient criterion** to decide whether its entire unit orbit reaches the coefficientwise ray $x\equiv y\equiv(A+1)/2\pmod A$.

This assigns every ideal exactly one of three mathematical statuses:

- **Ray match:** a norm-$N$ generator has the required oriented ray, so this ideal belongs to the oriented class of $(\rho)$; an exact positive Type B anchor seed follows by WP09-025.
- **Oriented principal, wrong ray:** a generator of norm $N$ and the correct real-sign pattern exists, but none of its unit orbits reaches the required ray.
- **No oriented norm-$N$ generator:** no such generator exists. This does not assert ordinary nonprincipality: generators of norm $+|N|$ or a different sign pattern are not ruled out.

**Proof.** Completeness of the ideal enumeration follows from Theorem A. Completeness of the representative enumeration follows from the protected WP09-023 fundamental-region argument. A representative belongs to its principal ideal of index $|N|$ and to no other ideal of that index. Two norm-$N$ generators of one ideal differ by a norm-one unit; with the prescribed real-sign orientation they lie on the positive unit orbit. WP09-026 exactly decides a ray hit on each such orbit. Thus every ideal is correctly classified. Resource guards raise rather than produce a partial negative classification. QED.

**Important limitation:** these classifications compute membership against one oriented ray class, **not** the full oriented ray class group, its group law or a general class-group presentation.
