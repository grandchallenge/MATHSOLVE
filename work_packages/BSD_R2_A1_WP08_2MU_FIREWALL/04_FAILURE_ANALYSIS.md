# Failure analysis

## FR-01 — equality after inverting 2 implies integral equality

**Disposition:** terminated. In `Z_2[[T]]`, localization at `2` erases the height-one `(2)` exponent. The exact residual factor is `2^delta`.

## FR-02 — a power of 2 is a harmless main-conjecture unit

**Disposition:** terminated. `2` is not a unit in `Z_2[[T]]`; its exponent is the Iwasawa mu contribution and changes the target valuation.

## FR-03 — Kato's p=2 Euler-system machinery automatically controls `(2)`

**Disposition:** terminated as a mechanical inference. The screened primary height-one statements explicitly omit the prime containing `2` at the relevant p=2 stage.

## FR-04 — Kato proves nothing at p=2

**Disposition:** terminated in the opposite direction. p=2 Euler-system/cotorsion applications exist. The missing claim is specifically the integral `(2)` component and the required equality/reciprocity, not the existence of all p=2 machinery.

## FR-05 — Greenberg mu=0 can be assumed from irreducible E[2]

**Disposition:** terminated. No general selected-class p=2 theorem was located in the current screen, and conjectural mu vanishing is not a proof premise.

## FR-06 — WP07 local correction equals the global mu defect

**Disposition:** terminated. The WP07 factor is a local interpolation multiplier with a known valuation. The WP08 delta is the relative height-one `(2)` exponent of global Iwasawa elements. They are different invariants.

## FR-07 — p=2 Heegner results for CM curves or bounded twist families close the non-CM target

**Disposition:** quarantined to their stated classes. They demonstrate p=2 feasibility but do not cover the selected uniform non-CM hypotheses.

## FR-08 — absence from the literature screen proves nonexistence

**Disposition:** terminated. The only warranted statement is that no qualifying general theorem was found in the current screen.

## FR-09 — an unverified manuscript can override primary-source scope

**Disposition:** terminated. Extraordinary claims must survive exact theorem-hypothesis reconciliation and governed source admission before theorem use.

## Reopening rule

A failed route reopens only with an exact proof or admitted source that controls the named missing exponent/reciprocity term under unchanged selected hypotheses.
