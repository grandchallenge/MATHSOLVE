## Strongest exact statement

The MRS fixed-infrared-cutoff ultraviolet-limit claim can be reduced to a small set of named proof nodes, but the 1993 paper does not close all of them at theorem grade. The large-field stability mechanism is the strongest closed part: Lemma II.1 is announced with a sketch and is explicitly completed by the Section VI stability estimate, culminating in Lemma VI.1 and (VI.35). By contrast, exact tuning of the relevant mass counterterm is stated as something that “should be solved” by methods used for infrared scalar phi^4_4; the all-scale effective-coupling/Mayer argument is referred to Rivasseau [R] and the Gross-Neveu construction [FMRS2] as a method template; Section VII expressly “summarize[s] the reasons for convergence” rather than giving a complete polymer-summability theorem; and Section VIII closes by calling the argument a “sketch of proof” while asserting that the Slavnov correction delta_N(rho) tends to zero.

No material missing Yang-Mills step located here is cleanly reduced by MRS to a cited prior theorem whose hypotheses visibly include the MRS four-dimensional pure SU(2), fixed-infrared-cutoff, regularized axial/background-gauge construction. The cited scalar phi^4_4 and two-dimensional Gross-Neveu results prove analogous constructive mechanisms in different models and limit problems, so they cannot by themselves be promoted to theorem authority for the MRS use.

## Claim boundary

This reconstruction establishes only the proof-dependency status of the source-stated ultraviolet removal at fixed infrared cutoff. It does not remove the infrared cutoff; establish infinite-volume Yang-Mills; prove the complete Osterwalder-Schrader axioms or OS positivity for the MRS limit; cover nontrivial topology or large gauge transformations; establish a physical mass gap; or constitute a Clay-style construction. Slavnov identities are not being identified with the OS axiom set. The scalar phi^4_4 and Gross-Neveu constructions are used only to identify what MRS cites and what those sources actually prove, not to transfer their theorems across models without a comparison theorem.
